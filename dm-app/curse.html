Or'Henn's Curse: Tiamat's Pull

Tracker for the side panels "Tiamat's Pull" and "Arc: Or'Henn and Tiamat". Say the number out loud whenever it changes. DM ruling 2026-11-08: the curse moves only to a dragon that willingly takes it.

How the Curse Works

(click to fold)

1 · What it is

Tiamat's mark sits in Or'Henn like a seed. It is not a wound: it is a delivery system. Its only goal is to make him ask a dragon for help. If he asks, and that dragon willingly takes it (DM ruling 2026-11-08), the curse jumps into the dragon, and when that dragon dies, Tiamat wakes in her true form. Tiamat cannot force him; she can only make asking feel like the kindest, safest thing in the world. Doing nothing is not safe: the number below only climbs.

3 · The Pull Save

Wisdom saving throw, DC 10 + Pull. He makes it:

  • In combat: at the start of each of his turns, if he can see a chromatic dragon or Tiamat.
  • Out of combat: whenever a dragon speaks to him.
  • Protection from evil and good on him: advantage.

On a failure, until the start of his next turn, the effect depends on the Pull at the moment he rolled:

0–3
Whispers		Disadvantage on his first attack against a chromatic dragon. His fire damage to chromatic dragons is halved.
4–6
The Leash		He can't target a chromatic dragon with an attack or harmful spell, and must spend half his movement moving toward the nearest one.
7–9
Kin		Charmed by the nearest chromatic dragon. On his turn he says aloud one line asking it for help. An ally within 5 ft. can stop the words with a reaction (grapple, a hand over his mouth, silence).
10
Ask		No save. He asks the nearest chromatic dragon for help.

So the climb is: distracted (Whispers) → can't fight them (Leash) → begs them (Kin) → begs with no save (Ask).

4 · What Counts as Asking

  • Any request for help to a dragon, in any language: "help me", "take it off me", "please". Saying yes to a dragon's offer of help (Vermilius offers on purpose).
  • Not asking: surrendering, bargaining, threatening, or asking a dragon a question.
  • Telegraph it: the moment before he speaks, he feels the mark lean toward the dragon like a hand reaching out. If he speaks anyway, enforce it.

5 · If a Dragon Takes It

  • The dragon must answer yes (its reaction) and willingly take it. A dragon can't be forced.
  • Or'Henn is free, fully. His gold scales stay gold.
  • The dragon is doomed: its eyes go flat gold, it stops fighting for itself and won't flee. The mark burns it out at the next dawn.
  • When it dies (killed, or at dawn), Tiamat wakes in her true form. Uldrak will kill it unless someone stops him. Keeping it alive (chains, hold monster) only buys hours.
  • The orb loophole: if he asks the dragon inside the orb of dragonkind, the orb becomes a gold cage. The curse leaves him, and Tiamat wakes only halfway, trapped in the glass. Don't hint at it until Small Vision 2 has come up.

6 · Gifts and Gold Scales

In visions, each of Tiamat's five heads offers one true gift. Accepting one: +1 gold scale and +2 Pull. Refusing costs nothing now ("Then we will talk again"), but the next vision comes sooner. The scales build up to 5: he can't refuse the next chromatic dragon that offers help. Remove curse and greater restoration don't touch them.

7 · Other Ways to End It

  • Her blood (the stolen vial): spent on Uldrak.
  • Her word: she lifts it if he kneels at the Monument as her champion, in Arkhan's place.
  • Wish: removes it, and the caster feels her look straight at them, once.
  • The dragon in the orb (above).

8 · One Round, Example

Pull 5, Flash in sight. Start of Or'Henn's turn: Wisdom save, DC 15. He rolls 9 total: fails, The Leash: he can't attack Flash this turn and moves half his speed toward him. Fight progression: Pull 6. Aurora uses her action to talk him back (Persuasion 17): Pull 5. Next turn, DC 15 again.
