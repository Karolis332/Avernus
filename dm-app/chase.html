Sky Chase: The Lady Vengeance Runs

Session 13 · Flash (red) and Vistalancer (blue) on the ship's tail. Homebrew band chase, written at the table on 2026-11-08. The full scene is in the Session 13 primer, "Sky Chase".

Gap · Round 1

Helm Roll (DC 15)

  • Success: one band away. Failure: one band closer. Fail by 5+: closer and a mishap.
  • Lost = escaped. Far 2 rounds in a row = Lost. Close 2 rounds in a row = caught (deck fight).
  • A round blinked out (Blink) is an automatic success. An Overclock fail or a Haste lethargy round is an automatic failure.

The Lady Vengeance

  • AC 17 (19 with Haste on the Core) · resists fire and poison · damage threshold 15: a hit of 14 or less does nothing.
  • One hit of 30+: roll a mishap. Bloodied at 175: 2d6 fire each round until Damage Control (DC 13).
  • Stations: Helm, Port cannon, Starboard cannon, Harpoon, Core. Jeremy: Maintain Core DC 15 every turn (fail: −10 ft. or a cannon dark).

Effects Running

The Chasers

  • Far: close in, no attack. Near: breath if recharged, else close in. Close: claws, bite, Wing Attack on the deck; Flash may land.
  • Wingover (each, once a round): the first ship-weapon attack on each dragon has disadvantage. Aim both cannons at one dragon. Faerie fire or guiding bolt cancels it.
  • Legendary Resistance 1/day each: the first big control spell will be shrugged off.
  • Both have blindsight 60 ft.: darkness and fog only hide the ship at Near or Far.

Log

    Casting Into the Ship (table ruling)

    • Scroll Feed. The engineer feeds a spell scroll of 2nd level or lower into the Core as an action: the spell takes effect on the whole ship, no check. If Jeremy does it, he skips Maintain Core that turn (counts as a failure).
    • Cast through the Core. A caster with a hand on the Core can make the ship the target of a spell that targets itself or one creature. Jeremy guides it in with an Intelligence (Arcana) check: DC 11 for 1st level, DC 13 for 2nd, DC 15 for 3rd. On a failure the spell still works but the Core flares (3d6 fire to whoever is at the Core at the start of each of their turns, until Damage Control). A movement spell lands 1d4 × 10 ft. off target instead and does not move the gap.
    • Concentration stays with the caster. The dragons know who to aim at.
    • Healing spells do not repair the ship (she is an object). Only Repairs and Damage Control do.
    • Ground spells (on the ground or a surface) do not work in open sky; on the deck they move with the ship. Glyph of warding breaks if the ship moves more than 10 ft.
