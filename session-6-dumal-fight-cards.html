Bane of the Bloodline. Her weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 radiant against fiends and Bhaalspawn, and she has advantage on saves against their abilities.

The Watch Holds (Aura). Allies within 15 ft. have advantage on saves vs. frightened and vs. Bhaal/fiend effects. An ally who would normally auto-fail such an effect (Drenwal vs. Bhaal's Whisper) may instead roll — with advantage.

Vigil-Weary (optional). Just woke from stone: Round 1 only, disadvantage on her first attack.

Actions

Multiattack. Veska makes two longsword attacks.

Crimson Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 2) slashing, plus 1d8 radiant vs. a fiend or Bhaalspawn.

Radiant Smite (3/Day). On a weapon hit, deal an extra 3d8 radiant — or 4d8 vs. a fiend or Bhaalspawn, who must also succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

Bonus Actions

Sergeant's Rally. One ally within 30 ft. who can hear her gains 1d8 + 3 temporary hit points and may immediately repeat one saving throw against an effect on them.

Reactions

Warding Oath. When a fiend or Bhaalspawn attacks an ally within 10 ft., impose disadvantage on the attack.

At the table. The field commander — stays near Drenwal, keeps the aura up, smites Dumal, and rallies the gun crews (treat a rallied gunner's cannon shot as +2d8).

Siege Monster. Deals double damage to objects and structures — i.e., the ship's hull.

Siege Hide. Takes half damage from Medium or smaller creatures. The cannons, big spells, and Titan-scale effects do full. This is why the ship has to kill it.

Tide of the Slain. When any creature dies within 30 ft., the Hecatomb regains 15 hit points and may vomit one Slaughter-Spawn onto a surface within 30 ft.

★ Soul-Furnace Heart (Weak Point). While hidden, the Titan keeps its resistances. The Aether Harpoon (or a called shot, DC 18 attack) tears its chest open, exposing the Heart for 2 rounds: while exposed it loses Siege Hide and all resistances, and attacks against it have advantage and deal +50% damage. Harpoon, then broadside.

Actions

Multiattack. Two slams, or one slam and Engulfing Grasp.

Slam. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft. Hit: 33 (4d12 + 9) bludgeoning (doubled vs. the ship).

Hurl the Dead. Ranged Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, range 120 ft. Hit: 27 (5d8 + 9) bludgeoning, and a Slaughter-Spawn rises where it lands.

Engulfing Grasp. The Hecatomb seizes the Lady Vengeance. The ship is grappled; while held, at the start of each of the Titan's turns the hull takes 6d10 bludgeoning and the deck tilts (everyone aboard DC 15 Dex or fall prone / slide). Escape: deal 60 damage to the gripping arm, or a DC 16 vehicle maneuver.

Charnel Roar (Recharge 6). 60-ft. cone: DC 16 Constitution save, 35 (10d6) necrotic, half on a success; creatures that fail are deafened for 1 minute.

Titan Actions (3/round)

Trample (1). Move up to half its speed.

Slam (2). One slam attack.

Raise the Slain (2). 1d3 Slaughter-Spawn claw out of its body.

Magic Resistance. Advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects.

Siege Monster. Deals double damage to objects and structures — it eats the hull.

Demonic Hide. While Dumal's binding-chains are intact, it takes half damage from Medium or smaller creatures (cannons/spells/harpoon do full). The Aether Harpoon (or DC 18 attack vs. a chain) shears a chain, exposing raw flesh for 2 rounds: it loses Demonic Hide and resistances and is struck at advantage and +50% damage.

Slipping Leash. Each chain sheared (and the first time it's bloodied) raises its Frenzy: +1 Flail head on Multiattack, and at the start of its turn it makes a DC 13 WIS save or attacks the nearest creature regardless of side — including Dumal's forces. When the last chain breaks, it is fully loose and attacks everything. The players' out.

Insatiable. When it reduces a creature to 0 HP, or a creature dies within 15 ft., it regains 20 HP and gains 1 Frenzy.

Brood of the Maw. Once per round, when a creature dies within 30 ft., a Hyena-Fiend claws up within 30 ft.

Actions

Multiattack. One Flail of Yeenoghu and one Devouring Bite.

Flail of Yeenoghu. Lashes up to three different targets within 20 ft. Each: +17 to hit. Hit: 22 (3d8 + 10) bludgeoning, and DC 18 STR or knocked prone and flung 15 ft.

Devouring Bite. +17 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 30 (4d10 + 10) piercing. A Large or smaller target reduced to 25 HP or fewer can be swallowed (blinded, restrained, 21 (6d6) acid/turn; escape by 25 damage from inside [gut AC 14] or DC 18 STR).

Rend the Hull. Smashes a structure within reach: automatic, 39 (6d12) bludgeoning (doubled vs. the ship). Its preferred target most rounds.

Butcher's Roar (Recharge 5–6). 60-ft. cone: DC 18 DEX, 45 (10d8) slashing, half on a success; failures knocked prone.

Legendary Actions (3/round)

Stride (1). Move up to half its speed. · Lash (2). One Flail head attack. · Feed (1). Bite a creature below half HP within reach. · Call the Brood (2). 1d3 Hyena-Fiends.

Insanely powerful, not a TPK. It spreads damage (3 small hits, not one delete), prioritizes the hull and the nearest thing over focus-fire, and its slipping leash turns it on Dumal's own forces. Beat it with the ship: harpoon a chain → broadside the exposed flesh → Overdrive to finish. Free it entirely and it savages everything.

The Lady Vengeance — the ship as a weapon

The Lady VengeanceGondian-Portyr Hellship
Gargantuan vehicle · the battlefield and the only thing that can fell the Titan
Armor Class 17
Hit Points 350 (Bloodied 175 = listing, on fire · 0 = sinking into Avernus)
Weapons & Systems

Arcane Cannons ×2. A gunner (crew or PC) fires one as an action: 8d10 force, range 600/2400 ft. A focused broadside on the exposed weak point ends the Titan fast.

Aether Harpoon. 4d12 piercing + tether. On the Titan it tears the weak point open, exposing it for 2 rounds (no resistances; struck at advantage and +50% damage). The key that unlocks the kill.

Soul Coin Overdrive (1/fight). Jeremy burns a soul coin: for 1 minute, ship weapons +2d10, speed ×2, 30-ft. force aura. Risk: d20, on 1–5 the Core backlashes (8d10 to the ship, engines down 1d4 rounds). Moral cost: a soul is annihilated to win.

Maintain Core (Jeremy). Action each round, DC 15 Arcana/Tinker's tools: success keeps full power; failure drops a cannon or speed for the round. Protect Jeremy.

Damage Control. Crew or PC action, DC 13: repair 2d6 hull or douse a fire.

The kill puzzle. PC weapons = half vs. the Titan. Harpoon to expose the Heart → broadside it in the 2-round window → Overdrive to finish (if they'll pay the soul). Two clean windows fell it.
