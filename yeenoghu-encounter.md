## ▶ The one-breath summary

> They freed the unicorn, the dao left, and the fog tore open. **Dumal walked out riding Yeenoghu — the demon lord of slaughter — chained by a soul he sold to Mephistopheles and the authority of his father Bhaal.** You can't kill a demon lord at level 11. You don't have to. **Break the leash and the two titans turn on each other** — or **scuttle the Lady Vengeance and rip the Weave open** to hurl Yeenoghu back to the Abyss for a century. Either way, the brother showed Drenwal exactly what total surrender to power costs.

**This is a vehicle/siege set-piece, not a stand-up fight.** The party's power tonight is the **ship and the leash**, not their HP bars.

## ▶ Can this even happen? — the lore, one screen

A mortal-ascendant does **not** master a demon lord. Dumal isn't mastering one — he's the **anchor point of a borrowed binding**, and it's killing what's left of him.

| Pillar | Canon basis | What it grants Dumal |
|---|---|---|
| **Devils bind demons** | The Blood War — Avernus captures & chains demons as weapons [CANON] | Precedent: leashing a demon on this layer is normal warfare |
| **Mephistopheles' pledge** | Archduke of Cania, lord of hellfire & infernal arcana; the original soul-bargain [CANON] | The **apparatus** — the chain itself. Price: "all of him." |
| **Bhaal's authority** | God of murder; Yeenoghu = demon lord of *slaughter* (adjacent domains) [HOMEBREW link] | The **grip** — kill-commands Yeenoghu's nature obeys when *aimed at prey* |
| **The contradiction** | A Lawful-Evil **devil's** chain on a Chaotic-Evil **demon** = Blood War aggression | The **crack**: the leash is unstable by its own logic → the 3-way fight |

**Ruling:** the binding holds for *minutes, not eternity*, and only while Yeenoghu is pointed at something he already wants to kill. Show it fraying from Round 1. **This is lore-tight precisely because it's doomed.**

## ▶ Design thesis — three ways to win, none of them "deal 333 damage"

You **cannot** grind Yeenoghu's 333 HP. Tell the players this through the fiction early (the unicorn's beam barely scorches him; Lulu *knows* what he is). Give them **three outs** and let them improvise across all of them:

| # | The out | How | Cost / Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Break the leash → 3-way fight** ⚔️ | Destroy the **Chain of Bhaal-and-Cania** (radiant/force/banishment) OR break Dumal's concentration on the Reins | Unleashes a **full CR 24** demon lord for a few rounds — survivable only with an exit |
| **2** | **Drop the rider** ⚔️ | Unhorse/bloody **Dumal** to his retreat threshold | He flees in Bhaal Smoke (arc continues) — *unless* the leash broke first, then Yeenoghu may catch him |
| **3** | **Scuttle the ship, rip the Weave** 💥 | **Jeremy's Gondian overload** — 3-round prime, then detonate | **Lose the Lady Vengeance.** Banishes Yeenoghu 100 years. The desperate trade. |

**Win state:** Yeenoghu gone (banished or recalled), Dumal driven off or slain (your Dial), the party bleeding but alive, and a new archdevil watching them from Cania.

## ▶ The set-piece — round-by-round flow

### Round 0 — the reveal (no rolls, pure dread)

Read the cliffhanger. Yeenoghu rises out of the fog: **Huge in the book — run him Gargantuan tonight**, swollen on Bhaal-blood and Mephistophelean hellfire, a chain of **red murder-light braided with blue hellfire** running from Dumal's chest to the demon's throat. Let the party *see the leash* — it's the most important object on the board.

> **Dumal:** *"Don't look at him. Look at me. I found the only thing in three Hells big enough to drag you home, Den — and I paid for it with everything I had. There's not much of me left to argue with. So let's not argue."*

### Round 1 — establish the leash holds, and that it hurts

- **Dumal** opens. Bonus action **Reins of Slaughter** → points Yeenoghu at the highest-damage PC or the ship. Yeenoghu takes **one** flail attack (leashed throttle). Dumal himself acts on Drenwal (Bhaal's Whisper / Lance).
- **The Warden of Cania** drops from the smog and makes straight for the **ship** — the one enemy a grounded Yeenoghu can't reach. It strafes the gunners and **interposes for the Chain.** Killing or displacing it is the party's mid-fight objective. → [block](#warden)
- **The unicorn** (if steered, DC 12) fires its beam — and it **barely marks Yeenoghu** (he shrugs radiant): *telegraph that the demon is unkillable, but the **chain** flares and screams when the beam crosses it.* **This is your tutorial: aim at the chain.**
- **Lulu** (DM voice): *"You can't kill the Beast of Butchery, child. But that chain — that's devil-work on demon-flesh. It hates itself. Break the chain and let them tear each other apart."*

### Rounds 2–4 — the grind on the leash / the ship dance

- Party splits into **ship roles** ([Lady Vengeance](#ship)) and **leash-cutters.** Cannons (force) + unicorn/Moonbow/Drenwal radiance pour into the **Chain**; **Leash Strain** climbs.
- **Yeenoghu is grounded (Speed 50, no fly).** A fast-flying ship can kite him; his only reach to the air is **Hurl Flail (60 ft)** and Dumal's spells. **Reward smart positioning** — staying 70+ ft up and raining cannon fire is the correct read.
- Each time Dumal takes damage, **concentration save on the Reins** (DC 10 or ½ damage). Break it → Yeenoghu acts on his *own* hunger for a round (still leashed, but wild) and **Strain +15**.
- **Strain milestones** ([Chain](#chain)): at **100**, Yeenoghu lashes a free flail at **Dumal** (telegraph!). At **160**, his aura flares against the rider (LOUD telegraph — *get airborne*). At **200 / Integrity 0**, **the leash shatters.**

### The pivot — UNLEASHED ⚔️ {#pivot}

The chain detonates in red-and-blue light. Yeenoghu throws his head back and **screams a sound that turns the gnolls of Avernus toward him for a hundred miles.** Then he looks down at the man who chained him.

> **Yeenoghu** (he does not speak words — he *broadcasts*): **HUNGER. BETRAYAL. THE SMALL THING DARED.**

- **Yeenoghu's first unleashed action: full Multiattack on DUMAL.** Three flails. ~60–90 dmg + frighten. This likely drops or near-drops the brother.
- **Dumal's response** → the **Dial** ([below](#dial)): Bhaal Smoke escape (arc continues) **or** the demon lord catches him (arc-ending).
- **The party's window:** flee on the ship · bombard Yeenoghu while he's busy with Dumal · trigger the scuttle · pile on Dumal to *claim the kill themselves* · extract anyone down.
- **After Dumal:** Yeenoghu rounds on the nearest living thing. Start the **Recall Timer** — see [aftermath](#aftermath). Now they NEED an exit.

**Actions**

**Legendary Actions (3/turn) — UNLEASHED ONLY.** *(Leashed: Dumal spends these as Reins commands; Yeenoghu has none of his own.)*

> **🎚 THE THROTTLE — why a CR 24 doesn't TPK a level-11 trio.** While the leash holds, Yeenoghu is *reined*: **one** flail attack per round (on Dumal's chosen target), **no** Legendary Actions, **no** Legendary Resistance against effects that target the **Chain**, aura = frighten only. He fights at roughly **CR 17** pressure. The full block above is what they unleash — deadly, brief, and aimed at Dumal first.

## ▶ The break-free 3-way fight — running it {#threeway}

When the leash shatters, this is the spectacle the whole night was built toward. Keep it fast and cinematic.

1. **Yeenoghu vs Dumal first.** Full Multiattack on the rider. The demon lord does not negotiate — it *corrects an insult.*
2. **Resolve Dumal** (the [Dial](#dial)). Smoke-and-gone, or torn apart.
3. **Yeenoghu turns on the field.** Roll him into initiative. Now he's a **full CR 24** with Legendary Actions and the gnoll-rising aura. **The party must already be leaving or detonating** — do not let them think they can now "finish" him.
4. **Recall Timer** ([aftermath](#aftermath)) — he is not here forever. Give the table the clock so the escape feels winnable, not hopeless.

**Party choices in the 3-way (all valid, all metered):**
- **Flee** on the ship while titans clash — clean exit, arc intact.
- **Bombard Yeenoghu** while he's busy with Dumal — chip the demon, risk drawing his eye.
- **Claim the kill on Dumal yourselves** instead of letting Yeenoghu do it — *very different meter & emotional outcome* (the brother dies by your hand, not the god-beast's).
- **Scuttle** to banish Yeenoghu *and* catch Dumal in the blast.
- **Save someone** — a downed PC, Jeremy, the unicorn.

## ▶ Jeremy's Gondian Overload — the nuke 💥 {#scuttle}

Jeremy (Gondian gnome — the artificers who **built the Hellships** [CANON]) can force the **weave engine** past containment: a deliberate arcane-core meltdown. The ship's already strained from the Palace; the failsafe is *right there.*

**Prime sequence — 3 rounds.** Each round Jeremy (or a PC at the engine) commits the round; the ship can still fly/fire but at **−1 cannon** while priming. **Telegraph hard:** runes screaming, soul coins boiling in their slots, the hum climbing to a shriek. The enemy can target the engine room (AC 17 section, 60 HP) to interrupt.

## ▶ Read-aloud narration (DScryb-grade) {#readaloud}

*Second-person, present-tense boxed text tuned to the DScryb house voice. Read slow. Let the silences breathe.*

### (1) The Reveal
> The smog peels back like a wound deciding to open, and a man walks out of it on foot for the length of one breath — then the ground behind him rises, and you understand he was never walking. He is *riding*. Ash sifts from a coat the color of old blood. His face is your cleric's face aged ten cruel years, the same jaw, the same eyes, and he is smiling the way you smile at someone you have missed. In his fist trails a chain that hums two notes at once. "Den," he says, soft, almost grateful, and the thing beneath him lifts its three skulls to find the sound of that name.

### (2) Yeenoghu's Presence
> The stink arrives before the rest of it can — wet fur and split bone and something underneath that is just *appetite*, a smell with teeth. A cloud moves with the demon, and the cloud is flies, a living carrion haze that drinks the light and chews the air into a low electric drone. Three hyena skulls ride a body the size of a siege tower, each grin too long, each empty socket somehow watching you anyway. A three-headed flail drags furrows in the scorched rock. Where its shadow falls, your courage thins like cloth over a flame, and one ancient certainty presses flat against the inside of your ribs: *you are meat, and it has come a very long way to be fed.*

### (3) The Chain, Up Close
> Follow the leash and your eye refuses to settle, because the chain cannot decide what it is. One strand is murder given color — Bhaal's red, the deep arterial light of a killing that never finished. The other is Cania's blue, a cold so total it reads as fire, hellflame burning at the bottom of the world where heat goes to die. They braid heart to throat, the man's chest to the demon's neck, and where the two lights touch they *hate* — they spit, they spark, they recoil and lash back together. It is not a leash. It is two damnations forced to share one rope, and somewhere under the snarl you can hear it: a fine, rising whine, like glass a half-second from the crack.

### (4) The Lady Vengeance Opens Her Broadside
> The deck pitches under your boots as Jeremy throws the lever, and the *Lady Vengeance* answers from the marrow of her hull. Force-cannons run out their throats along her flank with a sound like a cathedral inhaling. Then the world goes white-loud — *crack, crack, crack* down the line — and the recoil shoves the whole ship sideways through the smog. Bolts of compressed magic carve burning seams across the red sky and slam into the carrion cloud, scattering flies in spinning black gouts. Heat washes the railing. Cordite-bright arcane stink fills your mouth. For one fierce heartbeat the smoke is lit from inside like a thunderhead, and far below, the demon does not even flinch.

### (5) The Warden of Cania Drops from the Smog
> Something detaches from the overcast and *falls* — not clumsy, deliberate, wings of frost-plated bone snapping wide to catch the air a wingspan off the deck. The temperature drops so fast your breath becomes a ghost and the rigging crackles white. A bone devil sheathed in hoarfrost armor, every spur and rib rimed in glittering ice, its segmented tail curling over its head like a question with a fatal answer. It hangs between you and the chain — *its* duty, the leash its charge — and opens a mouth that should hold no breath at all. What comes out is winter: a roaring plume of killing cold that turns the rain to needles and frosts the cannon barrels solid mid-recoil.

### (6) The Leash Strains
> The two lights in the chain are losing their war. The red surges, the blue bites back, and the whole braid convulses — and that is when you feel the demon *change its mind*. Slowly, with a grinding of vertebrae like a ship's keel breaking, the leftmost skull turns. Not toward the *Lady Vengeance*. Not toward you. It rotates backward, down its own spine, until three sockets and one long grin are aimed squarely at the man riding its shoulders. The flies go quiet. The drone drops out of the air. Dumal's grin tightens at the corners. He hauls the chain two-handed, and the red light flares to drag the skull back around — but for one held breath, Yeenoghu looked at his rider, and Yeenoghu remembered what he is.

### (7) The Leash Shatters
> The whine in the chain climbs past hearing and the braid *lets go.* Red and blue tear apart in a single white scream, hellfire and murderlight flung in opposite arcs to gutter out against the rock, and the rope falls in two dead halves. For one enormous, ringing silence nothing moves. Then all three skulls come down at once, slow, deliberate, the way a god lowers its full attention onto a single ant — and Yeenoghu looks *down* at the small soft thing that bound him, rode him, dared. Dumal's chant dies in his throat. The flies turn from a haze into a hurricane. Three hyena mouths open as one, and the sound that pours out is the oldest sound there is — and now it is no longer aimed at you.

### (8) Jeremy Primes the Overload
> "She'll only do this once!" Jeremy is already wrist-deep in the weave-engine, gnome fingers flying across runes that have started to *glow wrong* — too bright, too fast, a heartbeat racing toward a stroke. The deck shudders. Down in the hull the contained magic that flies this ship begins to eat itself, and you hear it as a rising chord that climbs and will not stop climbing, every plate in the *Lady Vengeance* singing one ascending note of strain. Steam screams from her seams. The sigils smoke under his hands. Lulu's trumpet sounds a warning peal, high and bright and afraid. Jeremy looks back at you once, soot-black, eyes wide, and grins like a man lighting the last match in the world. "*Hold on to something.*"

### (9) The Detonation
> The note the ship has been climbing finally breaks — and the *Lady Vengeance* becomes a sun. There is no sound first, only light, a soundless white that erases the sky and prints the bones of your own hands across your eyes. Then the noise arrives all at once and shoves the air out of the world; the shockwave hits like a god's open palm and the heat comes behind it in a single rolling wall. At the heart of the glare the weave tears open — a ragged wound in the air itself, edges peeling into somewhere that is *not here* — and through it the demon is *dragged*, three skulls thrown back, the whole Gargantuan bulk of Yeenoghu folding sideways into the rift like water down a drain. ### (10) Aftermath A — Dumal Escapes in Bhaal Smoke

"I'd have shared it with you," he says, almost gentle, almost a boy again. ### (11) Aftermath B — Dumal Slain by Yeenoghu
> You see it happen and you will see it for the rest of your life. What was your brother becomes a single wet sound and a fine red mist that the carrion flies take with them into the dark, and then the Abyss swallows demon and remnant and all. The ash settles. There is nothing left to point it at.

### (12) Aftermath C — The Quiet
> The smoke thins by degrees, the way a fever finally breaks, and the red goes out of the sky one shade at a time. The carrion drone is gone. The cannons have stopped. Even the embers are surrendering their light, winking out across the black field one by one until the silence is total enough to hear your own pulse slowing in your ears. Wreckage of the *Lady Vengeance* ticks and cools somewhere behind you. Lulu's wings make a small, exhausted sound. And then, high overhead, the perpetual murk of Avernus parts just wide enough to show a single point of light — not a fire, not a spell, just one cold and distant star that has no business existing in this place. You stand in the ash and look up at it, and for the first time in a very long time, nothing is trying to kill you.

### Balance audit — the one fix that matters
**The original Chain numbers (Integrity 90 / Strain 100 / +10 per hit / +1 per 5 dmg) shatter the leash at the end of Round 1 by ~3×** — a full-party volley dumps ~117 Strain and ~300 Integrity damage in a single round, handing the party an *unleashed CR 24* right after they've spent their alpha strike on the Chain. TPK setup, not a 4-round set-piece. **Corrected values (already applied to the [Chain block](#chain)):** Integrity **300** (backstop only), Strain ceiling **200**, **+6** per radiant/force/banishment hit, **+1 per 8** Integrity damage, milestones **100 / 160 / 200**. With realistic *split* fire (≈2 cannons + unicorn + 1 striker on the Chain while the rest handle Yeenoghu / the Warden / gnolls) the leash now breaks **Round 4–5.** Two locks that keep the unleash from being a wipe: **Yeenoghu has no fly in BOTH modes** (canon — a grounded demon lord cannot chase the airborne ship), and **telegraph the shatter at Strain 160** so everyone can climb. The unleash becomes a *get-airborne sprint*, not a TPK.

> **Survivability (leashed, ~4 rounds):** sustained incoming ≈ 55/round vs ~40 healing (Drenwal + Lulu + unicorn) across ~335 front-line HP + the 350-HP ship — net ~15/round, fine. Expect **one knockdown** on the R3 spike (Yeenoghu flail + Dumal's Hellfire Pillar + Warden Hoarfrost on one body ≈ 80). That's drama, not a wipe. **Warden** (189 HP, AC 21) dies to focused cannon fire in ~1.5 rounds — its job is the once-per-round Interposition *tax*, not a sponge; don't buff it. **Scuttle:** Jeremy's engine room (AC 17 / 60 HP) survives the 3-round prime *if* the party screens 3+ gnolls away from it.

## ▶ Boss-fight design audit (7-step framework) {#audit}

1. **A boss worth the name** ✓ — Dumal is *integral* (the player's own brother), has a clear motive (drag "Den" home and "save" him by remaking him), a personality that escalates, and a stand-out kit (riding a demon lord on a soul-bought leash). Yeenoghu is the spectacle; **Dumal is the heart.**

6. **The boss feels real start-to-finish** ➕ — see the **75/50/25% ladder** below (sequences the [quote lines](#quotes)).
