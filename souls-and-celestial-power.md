# Souls, Soul Coins & Raw Celestial Power

Lore + mechanics for soul coins, freeing trapped souls, and the celestial liquid from the Demon Zapper. **[CANON]** / **[HOMEBREW]**. See also [[drenwal-bhaal-blood]].

## Soul coins — what they are
Each coin = **one real damned mortal**, conscious and suffering, forged into infernal currency. It can be **queried** (answers like *speak with dead* — how Rasheem interrogated Bloodbath). [CANON]

| Mode of use | The soul's fate |
|---|---|
| **Traded / carried** | Persists, trapped — reversible |
| **Spent as fuel** (war machine 24h, Soul Coin Overdrive, Gondian Core, drained dry) | **Annihilated** — true final death. No afterlife, no resurrection, not even *Wish.* |

**The horror:** every Soul Coin Overdrive / fueled war machine **erases a person forever.** Play it as such for Drenwal.

## Freeing souls — the Rite of Un-Coining [HOMEBREW]
- **Revive to life:** all but impossible (bodies are dust) — only *Wish* / a god.
- **Free to rest or judgment** (achievable): break the binding and send the soul on. Freed souls go to **judgment** (not guaranteed paradise); **innocents** go to rest. Cost: slow (one at a time), forfeits the coin's value, angers devils, raises **Coram/Eldath**.

**Methods:** Lulu (safe), the Moonkite (powerful), Drenwal's holy rite (risky — a botch **shatters/annihilates** the soul), Jeremy's Gondian un-coining, or carrying coins to a holy site / Plane Shift to a good plane.

### Lulu — the Trumpet of Release (the safe shepherd)
- Wraps a coin in radiance, sounds a note, ushers the soul on. **No shatter risk.**
- Auto for ordinary souls; **DC 13** (16 for archdevil-forged) for hard ones. **~2–3 per long rest** cleanly; each beyond costs **1 level of exhaustion.**
- **Conduit cost:** she relives the soul's last memory — draining, but it **jogs her own lost memories** (her healing arc; ties to **Lulu's Sister's Feather**). Freeing the wicked hurts her; freeing an innocent **restores** her.

### The Moonkite — Starpath / Starfire (powerful, rougher)
- **Starpath:** lights a lost/confused soul's way out (its *specialty* — it guides). **Starfire:** burns a deeply-bound coin open (raw power — can crack archdevil coins Lulu can't).
- **Scorch risk:** its fire can annihilate a fragile soul on a botch; it doesn't shepherd, so freed souls can wander or be re-claimed.
- **Cost:** **Vitality** (its resource; recovers slowly) + Aurora channels through the Moonbow.
- **Gate:** **assist-only** until Aurora resolves the Moonkite's **lost-patron / true-name** thread; solo soul-freeing unlocks then (evolving).
- **Best pairing:** Moonkite cracks + lights, **Lulu** shepherds → frees *anything* safely.

## Tears of the Caged Star — RAW CELESTIAL POWER [HOMEBREW]
The **unrefined essence of the Upper Planes**, wrung from the caged unicorn by the Demon Zapper. Holy water is a faint rumor of this. The party is bottling the basin residue; a few drops grew a dead pot into a full plant.

- **Quality fork:** unicorn **freed (mercy)** = pure, full potency. **Died in the machine / forced** = grief-tainted (weaker, weeps, minor psychic on use).
- **What raw celestial power does (big verbs):**
  - **Create / restore life** — regrow flesh & limbs, call back the recently dead, green dead land, cure any disease/poison/curse.
  - **Illuminate / reveal** — shatter illusion, force true forms (no fiend hides), expose lies & fate.
  - **Judge / unmake** — *annihilates* the corrupt; **severs infernal bindings** (soul coins, pacts, the Zapper).
  - **Empower / ascend** — grant celestial traits (flight, radiance, truesight); fully **restore the Moonkite**; supercharge Lulu.
  - **Consecrate** — sanctify ground, objects, a person.
- **The price of RAW power:**
  - **Burns the unclean — including Drenwal.** Pour it on him and the Bhaal blood makes it **flare and burn him.** *He can't use it on himself.*
  - **Overloads** mortals (blindness, radiant burns, or being undone into light).
  - **Volatile** (can flare/detonate/evaporate); **has a conscience** (recoils from cruelty); a **beacon** (draws fiends & greater powers).
- **Channeling tiers:** *a drop* (controlled, via conduit) / *a vial* (one big thing — wake Veska, regrow Lucan, restore the Moonkite, blast a soul-coin, banish a fiend) / *all of it* (a one-time miracle; the channeler risks being consumed).
- **Conduit rule:** safest stepped-down through a celestial (**Lulu** or the **Moonkite**). A mortal channeling it raw gambles with the divine.
- **Bottling:** it bonds to **living / once-living / blessed vessels** (a plain bottle leaks within a week); **DC 13 Nature/Arcana** to stabilize (Lulu/Moonkite blessing = auto). Yield **~1d4+1 doses** + the restored plant (a minor celestial bloom). **Finite — a mercy-currency.**
- **Unlocks across the arc:** the catalyst for **Veska's Vigil-break**, **Lucan's** clean leg-fuel / regrown flesh, **Moonkite** Vitality, **hard soul-freeing**, the sigil's Kept Watch road. **Cannot purge the Bhaal blood.**
