Cold Open — The Detour

Where this fits The party has chosen to detour from Vix's parley invitation to rescue the trapped unicorn at the Demon Zapper landmark. They've burned 20–45 minutes of travel time to reach the site. Vix has not yet vanished — he's tagging along, polite and curious, perched somewhere visible. He has not yet reported back to Dumal.
The wind off the wastes carries the smell of ozone — not the lightning kind. The sharp celestial kind, like a temple after rain. It does not belong in Avernus, and that is how you know you're close. You see the device before you see the road. Four iron-and-bone spires stand in a square fifty feet across, each two stories tall, lashed with copper wire and red-orange runes that pulse like a slow heartbeat. Between them, suspended on chains: an iron cage. Inside the cage — A unicorn. Silver-white. Its mane is half torn out. A harness of black iron pierces the base of its horn and feeds back down its neck. The horn glows. The spires glow with it. Beneath the cage: an iron crucible the size of a barrel, half-full of a substance that looks like liquid starlight — sluggish, dimming. Two barbed devils stand at opposite spires. One is sharpening its claws on the iron. The other looks bored. The unicorn sees you. Its eyes are still its own.

Terrain notes

  • The square is 50 ft wide. Spires at the corners. Cage centered. Crucible under the cage.
  • The cage is 20 ft up, suspended by 4 chains running diagonally to the spire tops.
  • Each spire's runes pulse on a 6-second beat. Round-by-round, the unicorn loses 2 HP from the harness while the device is active. This is the timer.
  • The unicorn enters the encounter at 40/67 HP (already wounded). 20 rounds before death if nothing changes.
  • Open ground for 200 ft around. No cover. Devils have line of sight on anyone approaching unless stealthed.
Death ClockThe unicorn dies if HP hits 0. Each round of inaction = −2 HP. If the spires are pushed harder (devils overload the device, see Combat), it goes to −5/round.

The Cage Puzzle — Three Locks

How to run thisThe puzzle has three independent locks. Each one spotlights a different PC. They can be worked in parallel (one PC per lock) or serially. The unicorn loses 2 HP per round while ANY spire is still active — this is the timer. Total puzzle time at 3 PCs working parallel: ~3–5 rounds.

Lock 1 — The Rhythm of the Spires DEX / Arcana spotlight

The four spires pulse clockwise, in sequence, on a six-second beat. At any moment, one spire's rune is bright (hot, lethal to touch) and the other three are dim (vulnerable). The pattern is observable.

  Beat 1:  [BRIGHT]  [dim]      Beat 3:  [dim]    [dim]
           [dim]     [dim]               [dim]    [BRIGHT]

  Beat 2:  [dim]     [BRIGHT]   Beat 4:  [dim]    [dim]
           [dim]     [dim]               [BRIGHT] [dim]

How to find the pattern

  • DC 12 Perception — notice the runes light in sequence, not all at once
  • DC 14 Arcana — recognize this as a binding circle; it must be broken at opposite corners (diagonal pair: 1+3 OR 2+4), not adjacent. Breaking adjacents allows the circle to re-route through the other diagonal.
  • DC 13 Investigation on a spire — the rune is a temporary etching that fades briefly between pulses; touch must land in the dark moment

Disabling a spire

Action on your turn. Player declares which spire AND which initiative count they touch it (timing-as-mechanic — this is the puzzle, not just a roll).

State of rune when touchedResult
Dim (correct timing)DC 13 Sleight of Hand OR Arcana. Success: spire goes dark. Failure: nothing happens, try next round.
Bright (mistimed)Backlash: 2d6 radiant to the toucher. The device pulses harder — unicorn loses 4 HP that round instead of 2. No skill check.
Lock 1 solved when: two opposite spires (diagonal pair) are disabled. The pulsing stops. Cage chains slacken but do not release — the harness still holds.

Lock 2 — Calming the Unicorn CHA / Animal Handling spotlight

Even with all spires dead, the iron harness pierces the unicorn's horn with three barbs. Yanking it would tear the horn out and kill her. She must be calmed first — she has been in the cage forty-one years, and the last thing that approached her was a barbed devil.

The calm check

  • DC 15 Persuasion / Animal Handling / Performance (singing).
  • Aurora has advantage if she shows the Moonkite — the celestials recognize each other.
  • Drenwal has DISADVANTAGE — he reeks of Bhaal to celestial senses. Anlein's eyes go wide when he approaches. This is a tragic narrative beat; lean into it.
  • Asimov has DISADVANTAGE — the Soul Capacitor / Rasheem is fiend-aligned. Anlein flinches from the lamp.
  • Lulu can intercede (1×/session). Automatic success on calming + costs Drenwal's "Lulu intercession" slot for the session (already −2 from highest meter per the v2 rules).

Failure of the calm check: the unicorn thrashes. Anyone touching her takes 2 (1d4) damage from the horn on her next reaction. Retry next round; calm DC drops by 1 each failed attempt (she's exhausted).

Once she is calm, the harness can be removed. Three barbs are embedded along the horn base. Each must come out cleanly.

Time Pressure — The Drain Clock

The device draws on Anlein every round until all spires are dark OR the harness is off, whichever comes first. Show the tracker bar to the players from minute one — make the timer visible.

  • Any active spire: unicorn loses 2 HP/round.
  • Mistimed touch (Bright rune): 4 HP that round.
  • Devil "Overload" action (Round 3+ if losing combat): 5 HP/round.
  • Failed calm + flailing: no additional HP loss, but the harness can't be removed until calmed.

Anlein enters the encounter at 40/67 HP. Math: she has roughly 20 rounds of grace under normal drain. Overload cuts that to ~8 rounds. Make the player feel the count.

If Combat Triggers

Initiative order suggestion

  1. Barbed Devil A — INIT 17 (DEX +3)
  2. Barbed Devil B — INIT 16
  3. Party (roll normally)
  4. The Device (no init — operates at end of round, drains unicorn 2 HP)

Barbed Devil tactics

  • Round 1: One climbs its spire (climb 30 ft, Athletics), starts lobbing Hurl Flame from elevated cover (+5 to hit, 3d6 fire, range 150 ft). The other engages the closest melee threat.
  • Round 2: If a spire goes dark, the climbing devil drops to defend it (free action via Barbed Hide — it's reckless and just falls, 2d6 self-damage, doesn't care).
  • Round 3+: If reduced below half HP, both devils target the unicorn directly — they're trying to break it on purpose to deny the rescue. "If we cannot keep, we burn." One Hurl Flame at the unicorn per round.
  • Barbed Hide trigger: Anyone melee-attacking a barbed devil takes 1d10 piercing reactively. Aurora at STR 3 — strongly disincentivize melee.
The Device as Hostile ObjectWhile both devils live and at least one spire is active, the device draws 2 HP from the unicorn at end of round. If devils declare "Overload" (action, both must agree, can't move that turn), the draw goes to 5 HP/round until the device is destroyed or a devil dies. They'll do this at Round 3+ if losing.

Encounter Math

  • 2 Barbed Devils @ CR 5 = 3,600 XP (Hard for L11 party of 3)
  • Add device drain = Deadly if combat extends past Round 4
  • Solution shape: 1 PC disables spires, 2 PCs hold the devils. Asplit is faster than attrition.

After the Rescue

The cage clatters open. The harness slides off the horn — its iron blackened where the spike pierced. The unicorn stands shakily on the ash-flat ground. It is taller than you remembered unicorns being. Its breath smells like cold mountain water. It walks, slowly, to whoever broke the device. It lowers its head. The horn touches that person's chest, light as a finger. The voice is in their head. Not loud. Old. "I have been here forty-one years. You are the first who tried."
Anlein: "I am called Anlein in the speech of my grove. Quiet Spring. I was taken from a roadside chapel in the Western Heartlands. My priestess prayed to the river. The river answered with you. I cannot stay. The devils know my horn now. They will hunt me here. I have one gift to give before I plane-step home. Choose carefully. I will offer only one."

Departure

Whichever gift she gives, Anlein casts the spell, looks once at Drenwal — "Walk in the spring's shadow, brother of a wolf" — and Plane Shifts away. She is gone in a flash of pale water-light. The crucible's last contents evaporate as a faint blue mist.

Aurora moment Aurora's Moonkite reacts to Anlein's departure — bows its head, tracks the direction the unicorn went. They are celestials of similar octave. If Aurora asks the Moonkite about it: she gets a wordless impression of kinship. The Moonkite knew Anlein, somewhere. Long ago. Seed for Moonkite-patron investigation.

Loot & Gifts Summary

  • Anlein's gift — one of (A) Plane Shift, (B) Heal + Curse-Break, (C) Eldath's Vouch (+15 devotional)
  • From the dead devils: 2 Soul Coins (1 per body). Their barbed hides are worth 50 gp each as armor reinforcement to a smith.
  • From the crucible (if not destroyed): The remaining celestial residue can be bottled (DC 14 Arcana to siphon, requires a glass vessel). One-use: coat a weapon for 1 minute → adds 1d8 radiant per hit. Or drink it for advantage on the next save vs a fiend-aligned effect within 24 hours. Single charge.
  • The harness — black iron, the kind that bound the unicorn. Worth nothing materially. If carried: a token of guilt. Drenwal touching it during a long rest = vivid dream of being the one in the cage. Plot prop.
  • The four spire-runes — if salvaged (DC 16 Arcana), each can fuel a fiend-arcane circle for 1 hour. Worth 25 gp to a Hellrider scholar or 50 gp to a fiend dealer.
