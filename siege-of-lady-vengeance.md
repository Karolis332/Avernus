# Siege of the Lady Vengeance — the Dumal Finale

An epic two-front battle: Dumal + boarders assault the ship while a titan wrecks the hull. **Statblock cards:** `session-6-dumal-fight-cards.md` (build → `.html`).

## Concept
Dumal's host marches on the *Lady Vengeance* across Avernus, a titan at the front. Two battles run at once:
- **The Deck** (personal): Dumal + Bone Devils vs. the party + Veska / Lucan / the Gith + crew.
- **The Siege** (vehicle): the titan vs. the ship's guns. **The ship is the only thing that can fell the titan; the heroes keep the gun crews alive.**

## ⚠ OPEN DECISIONS (lock before play)
- **The Titan:** Option A **The Hecatomb** (Bhaal charnel titan, CR 18) · Option B **The Maw of Yeenoghu** (Dumal's bound demon-beast, CR 20). **Recommended: the Maw** (ties to the Bhaal×Yeenoghu *Covenant of Slaughter*; its "slipping leash" can turn it on Dumal's forces = built-in not-a-TPK lever).
- **Avatar of Bhaal:** **PENDING** — Dumal's phase-2/3 transformation (a Gargantuan Avatar) is *not yet statted*. Intended finale: titan softens the ship → its leash slips → **Dumal ascends into the Avatar of Bhaal** for the climax.
- **Dumal Dial:** retreat (arc continues) vs. death (**Last Rites**). Decide before initiative.
- **Yeenoghu:** the demon lord stays **off-screen** (CR 24 = TPK). Dumal commands a *bound beast*, justified as a Bhaal×Yeenoghu covenant of slaughter (or simply a leashed Abyssal monster).

## The two-front engine
- **Deck:** Dumal + Bone Devils land; party + allies fight; **spined devils dive-bomb the gun crews.**
- **Siege:** the titan wrecks the hull; **PC weapons do half (Siege Hide)** — the cannons/spells/harpoon do full. **Kill puzzle: Aether Harpoon to expose the weak point → broadside it in the 2-round window → Soul Coin Overdrive to finish (at the cost of a burned soul).**

## Order of battle (scaled for the allies present)
- **Dumal** (CR 16 → Avatar of Bhaal finale) — boards, hunts Drenwal.
- **The Titan** (Hecatomb *or* Maw of Yeenoghu) — the siege threat.
- **Bone Devils ×2** — cover Dumal, hit the casters.
- **Spined Devils ×4–6** — exist to murder the gun crews / Jeremy.
- **Brood** (Slaughter-Spawn or Hyena-Fiends) — endless boarders spawned by the titan.
- *(Optional: Ralzala as a third-side wildcard.)*

## The ship as a weapon (canonical stats)
- **AC 17, HP 350 (DT 15). 0 HP = sinking into Avernus (TPK clock).**
- **2 Arcane Cannons** — 8d10 force, 600/2400 ft., a gunner each (crew or PC).
- **Aether Harpoon** — 4d12 + tether; *for this fight,* it also **shears the titan's weak point → exposes it for 2 rounds** (no resistances, hit at advantage/+50%).
- **Soul Coin Overdrive (1/fight):** +2d10 weapons, ×2 speed, 30-ft. damage aura. **Risk:** d20, 1–5 = 8d10 backlash + engine down 1d4 rounds. **Moral cost:** burns a soul (annihilated — see [[souls-and-celestial-power]]).
- **Jeremy — Maintain Core** (action/round, DC 15 Arcana/Tinker's). **Damage Control** (action, DC 13, repair 2d6 hull / douse fire).

## Phases
1. **Approach.** The host closes; spined devils strafe; first long-range volleys; choose Overdrive timing.
2. **Boarding.** Dumal + devils land (deck fight) while the titan reaches the hull (hurls dead, spews brood).
3. **The Grab / Rend.** The titan seizes or smashes the ship — deck tilts, hull crushed each round. Harpoon-and-broadside the weak point. Dumal goes **bloodied** (Avatar of Slaughter; begins ascending).
4. **Climax.** Titan felled; **Dumal → Avatar of Bhaal**; allies may fall heroically; the Dumal Dial resolves.

## Ally & crew roles
- **Lucan** — *Keep the Watch* shields the gun crews and Jeremy.
- **Veska** — her aura frees minds vs. Dumal (even Drenwal rolls vs. Bhaal's Whisper); **Sergeant's Rally buffs a cannon shot (+2d8)**; smite blinds the titan/Dumal.
- **The Gith** — blinks between cannons to man a vacant one; **Mind Lance** abuses the titan's / Dumal's weak INT; saves his **Stun** for the Avatar.
- **Crew** — the gunners (every one killed = a silent cannon). **Jeremy** — the Core, the Overdrive, and the soul question.

## Win / lose
- **Win:** titan felled (ship freed) **and** Dumal resolved.
- **Lose:** hull hits 0 → the *Lady Vengeance* sinks into Avernus. **Overdrive (a burned soul) is the desperate out** — the moral knife of the whole scene.
