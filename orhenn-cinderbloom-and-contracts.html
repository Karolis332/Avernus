Heirloom of the Ashen Garden

Cinderbloom, the Ashen Shepherd

Staff, very rare (requires attunement by a druid)

Living-ashwood, warm as sun-baked stone. Its grain twists upward into a cage of blackened branches cradling a fist-sized ember that burns without fuel or smoke — brightening whenever beasts draw near or roots stir beneath the earth. It serves as a magic quarterstaff and a druidic spellcasting focus.

Ashwood Weapon. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic quarterstaff, and a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls while you hold it.

Cinderheart. As a bonus action, expend 2 charges to send the ember streaking to a point within 60 ft. One creature within 5 ft of that point makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 3d8 fire damage on a fail, half on a success. A 10-ft-radius of smoldering roots erupts there, becoming difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. The flames ignite unattended flammables but never harm plants you choose.

Rootwright Magic. The staff has 10 charges. While holding it you can take a Magic action to expend charges and cast one of the following, using your spellcasting ability and your spell save DC:

  • Entangle — 1 charge
  • Spike Growth — 2 charges
  • Erupting Earth — 3 charges
  • Plant Growth — 3 charges
  • Wall of Fire — 4 charges

When cast from the staff, entangle, spike growth, and the overgrowth of plant growth can erupt black ashwood roots from bare stone, metal, bone, or the blasted soil of Avernus — no existing vegetation required. Such conjured growth crumbles to harmless ash after 8 hours.

Recharge. The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges when the attuned bearer finishes a long rest, but only once every 24 hours. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20; on a 1 the ember goes dark and the staff becomes a nonmagical quarterstaff until it is planted in fertile soil and watered continuously for 8 hours.

Keeper of Fell Beasts. You have advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks involving Beasts, Monstrosities, or Fiends of Intelligence 6 or lower. As a Magic action, expend 4 charges and present the ember to one such creature within 30 ft that can see it, provided its challenge rating is no higher than your proficiency bonus: it makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC (with advantage if currently hostile) or is charmed for 1 hour, treating you as its trusted keeper and obeying simple bonus-action commands unless obviously suicidal. Whenever it takes damage it repeats the save, ending the effect on a success; on a save or when the effect ends, the creature is immune for 24 hours.

The Mother Ash · Restoration

Cinderbloom Rekindled

Staff, legendary (requires attunement by a druid) — Cinderbloom's awakened form

The Mother Ash (Vaelisha), the Last Green of Avernus. Before this plane became the first layer of Hell it was a living world; when it fell, all its nature burned save one tree — hidden in a warded hollow, kept barely alive. Every infernal gardener's staff, Or'Henn's and his predecessor's alike, was cut from her boughs. To rekindle Cinderbloom the party must gather what remains of the nature of Avernus and feed it to the Mother Ash; her renewed sap re-infuses the staff.

The infusion (quest): collect three Green Remnants — e.g. the last seed of Idyllglen, living moss from a fallen titan's ribs in the Bleeding Wastes, and a sap-heart cutting held within Lady Urexiel's Scabrous Menagerie (ties into Contract V) — then perform an 8-hour rite at the Mother Ash. Cinderbloom becomes Cinderbloom Rekindled, keeping all its powers and gaining those below.

Greenfire Charges. Capacity rises to 14 charges; it regains 2d6 + 4 on a long rest and no longer risks going dark on a 1 — the Mother Ash's sap keeps the ember lit.

Deepwood Casting. Add to the Rootwright list: Wall of Thorns (5 charges), Sunbeam (6), Fire Storm (7). Cinderheart now deals 4d8, and against fiends its damage is radiant — the last nature of Avernus burns straight through devilish fire-immunity.

Bloom of the Last Green. Once per day, as an action, raise a living grove in a 30-ft radius for 1 minute: natural difficult terrain of ashwood roots and ember-flowers. At the start of each of your turns, allies within regain 1d8 hit points and any fiend that starts its turn there takes 2d8 radiant. You can see and target through it freely.

Warden's Hold. Keeper of Fell Beasts now affects creatures of Intelligence 8 or lower, and you may keep two such creatures charmed at once.

The Gardener's Due

IssuerProcurator Vhalzareth, Estate-Reclamation Advocate of the Third Infernal Chancery

The MarkReturn to the ruins of Or'Henn's former estate and recover its brass boundary seal from beneath the demolished western conservatory — intact and "substantially free of demonic residue."

The BountyAn authenticated Blood War salvage manifest naming the barlgura Kharzug the Root-Eater as the creature that removed Cinderbloom from the estate. No honest document gives a distance in Avernus; it fixes Kharzug's last deployment as the Weeping Furrow, known by three signs — broken chains crossing the sky, iron rain falling upward, and a trench that weeps black sap.

ExecutionBinding only once Or'Henn marks it in ash, the issuer countersigns, and Fhet'Ahla — Mahadi's amnizu notary — affixes the seal of the charter. Voidable only by both signatories before Fhet'Ahla, on surrender of the original and payment of his fee.

The EncumbranceRecovery of the seal confirms Or'Henn as the estate's final acting groundskeeper and assigns him custody of every escaped, unculled, or improperly interred specimen registered to its grounds. Three remain unaccounted for. At each infernal dawn a specimen stays uncatalogued, the seal gains one breach-mark; upon three, the Chancery may seize any property recovered under this instrument.

SOUL BOUNTY II · not binding until sealed & notarized

A Measure of Honest Fire

IssuerSerephine Coal-Tongue, salamander smith of the Gilded Crucible (an elemental — so this is a chartered commercial instrument, enforced only once notarized under Mahadi's charter)

The MarkHer forge is being poisoned by a rival's shipment of grave-cold iron. Enter the rival's warehouse, identify the contaminated ingots, and mark them with druidic flame. Nothing stolen, no employee slain.

The BountyShe binds one nonmagical weapon in infernal brass (granting a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls), or one druidic focus (granting a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls while held). In place of this service, Or'Henn may instead claim three cinderseed ampoules (an action to throw within 30 ft; entangle in a 20-ft square for 1 minute, save DC 15).

ExecutionBinding only once Or'Henn marks it in ash, the issuer countersigns, and Fhet'Ahla affixes the seal of Mahadi's charter. Voidable only by both signatories before Fhet'Ahla, on surrender of the original and payment of his fee.

The Encumbrance"Employee" excludes contractors, debtors, summoned creatures, animated tools, and any creature whose employment ended before its recorded moment of death. Every ingot Or'Henn marks is thereby certified defective; if any marked ingot is later adjudged sound, he owes one year and one day of furnace service — or three soul coins.

SOUL BOUNTY III · not binding until sealed & notarized

The Hound That Remembers

IssuerPellucid Jax, infernal beast broker of Jax's Immaculate Menagerie

The MarkCapture — alive and unmaimed — an ember-muzzled hell hound prowling beneath the Emporium's nightly route. It wears the remains of a green copper collar and answers to "Morrow." Jax claims it has eaten three customers and one technically irreplaceable ledger.

The BountyJax provides the key and authentication phrase that open the sealed inner plate of Morrow's collar. Beneath it lie the crest of Or'Henn's former household and a hardened flake of living ashwood: held over a flame, the flake bends toward the tree — or staff — it was cut from, a compass for 1 hour before burning to ash.

ExecutionBinding only once Or'Henn marks it in ash, the issuer countersigns, and Fhet'Ahla affixes the seal of Mahadi's charter. Voidable only by both signatories before Fhet'Ahla, on surrender of the original and payment of his fee.

The EncumbranceMorrow is registered under infernal law as an "independent commercial predator." Delivery therefore constitutes an unsolicited adoption unless 500 gp is deposited into the collar's escrow seal before Jax opens it. If the escrow remains unpaid, title to Morrow — and every recorded liability of its prior killings — vests in Or'Henn.

SOUL BOUNTY IV · not binding until sealed & notarized

Articles of Temporary Embodiment

IssuerMistress Nymira Quill, night hag and broker of posthumous opportunities (a hag — so Fhet'Ahla stands as enforcing notary)

The MarkObtain the still-warm shadow of a creature that willingly entered an infernal bargain and died regretting it — folded into the instrument before the corpse grows cold.

The BountyAsimov receives a spectral facsimile of his former body for 1 hour, using his normal statistics; spectral copies of his carried equipment function for him alone. Once during the rental he can touch one creature and perceive the exact text of one infernal contract binding it, and he learns one concealed clause engraved into the soul capacitor's own confinement instrument.

ExecutionBinding only once Or'Henn marks it in ash, the issuer countersigns, and Fhet'Ahla affixes the seal of Mahadi's charter. Voidable only by both signatories before Fhet'Ahla, on surrender of the original and payment of his fee.

The EncumbranceWhen the hour ends, the nearest eligible recipient takes necrotic damage equal to half the hit points Asimov lost during the rental, to a maximum of 8d10, ignoring resistance but never reducing the recipient below 1 hit point. An eligible recipient is any creature that knowingly accepted healing, inspiration, protection, food, or tactical counsel from Asimov during the rental.

SOUL BOUNTY V · not binding until sealed & notarized

The Orchard of Borrowed Names

IssuerAdvocate Malfius Nine-Reeds, lesser counsel to Fhet'Ahla and clerk of the Third Infernal Chancery, working a curtained stall with no visible entrance

The MarkA grove of iron trees has grown around a buried infernal war machine, feeding on the true names of those who approach. Cut a path to the machine and retrieve the obsidian coffer from its command seat. Do not open the coffer, and do not speak your own name while inside the grove.

The BountyA writ of reclamation recognized by Zariel's legions and every merchant under Mahadi's charter, unless countermanded by a pit fiend, infernal duke, or archdevil. It names Cinderbloom property of Or'Henn's inherited household and authorizes recovery from any registered scavenger, bonded mercenary, infernal unit, or chartered merchant holding it. Malfius further attests the staff passed through the Weeping Furrow and was presented as tribute to Lady Urexiel, Keeper of the Scabrous Menagerie.

ExecutionBinding only once Or'Henn marks it in ash, the issuer countersigns, and Fhet'Ahla affixes the seal of Mahadi's charter. Voidable only by both signatories before Fhet'Ahla, on surrender of the original and payment of his fee.

The EncumbranceThe writ names Or'Henn successor to his former master's estate, transferring every surviving asset, servant, enemy, debt, oath, lien, and unfinished sentence attached to that household. The coffer contains a blank name-substitution writ keyed to the Third Chancery; if it is opened, the first true name released replaces Or'Henn's name on every agreement notarized by that Chancery. Malfius declines to identify the name imprisoned within.
