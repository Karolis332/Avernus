# Drenwal's Bhaal Blood — Control, Sigils & the Two Cures

Homebrew arc mechanics for leashing, mastering, or purging the Bhaal blood in Drenwal. Tags: **[CANON]** sourcebook · **[HOMEBREW]** ours. See also [[hellrider-lore]], [[souls-and-celestial-power]].

## The two cures (the spine of his arc)
- **Mordenkainen's path — Rebirth.** Cure = *"die and be reborn in the eyes of a god who has never claimed you."* **Purges** the blood. The mercy road. Officiated by **Veska** (a Hellrider rite, under Tyr/Helm/Coram) — see `npc-reference.md`.
- **Veska's reveal — the Three Sigils.** Don't purge it — **master** it. The power road. Costs kin-blood and a kinslaying; may be Bhaal's design dressed as salvation.
- **These two cures compete.** How Drenwal uses the Sigil of the Kept Watch leans him toward one ending.

## Sigil of the Kept Watch — the leash (Sigil #1, DONE)
Drawn in the quill/Vigil scene (Aurora finished it while a Bhaal-seized Drenwal attacked her). Leashes the blood.

- **Always-on:** advantage on saves vs. Bhaal/fiend effects; immune to fear/charm from fiends & Bhaalspawn (flips his Dumal auto-fails into saves); passive **Bhaal-Sight** (Bhaalspawn within 1 mile, killers within 60 ft.); fiend hits halved once/turn; **Bhaal's Claim no longer rises from ambient temptation — only from using the powers below.**
- **Watch pool (3 charges/long rest):** *Sanguine Flame* (+2d8 necrotic; on a kill, heal 10 to an ally — Hunger Pact, sanctified); *Kept Watch aura* (10 ft.: +1 AC, advantage vs. fear/fiend); *Slaughter Stayed* (reaction: an ally drops to 1 instead of 0).
- **Mark of the Watch (1/long rest nova):** 1 min — +1 attack, +2 to hit, hits frighten (his DC). End: **DC 15 CON or the sigil cracks** (powers offline until re-consecrated).
- **Cost dial:** each charge → **Bhaal +2**; spend it to spare/protect → **Coram +1** instead. **Crack/relapse** if Bhaal's Claim crosses ~65, a cold-blood kill, or after the nova → Bhaal can seize him again; re-consecrate via Veska.

## The Three Sigils — the path to MASTER the blood (Veska's reveal)
Cult lore. The same ritual that forged **Dumal the Flame Ascendant.**

| Step | Sigil | Cost | = Sigil evolution tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Leash | first sigil *(done)* | discipline | Tier I (Kept Watch) |
| 2 — Binding | second sigil drawn **in another Bhaalspawn's blood** | a sibling's blood (Dumal's or Senna's) | Tier II |
| 3 — Mastery | **kill the last living Bhaalspawn** | a kinslaying | Tier III |

- **Siblings:** Drenwal, **Dumal**, **Senna**. To become sole master, both others must go — one bleeds (Step 2), one is killed last (Step 3).
- **The horror:** "the last one" may be **Senna** (the innocent child) → kinslaying = the deepest act of Bhaal. The Bhaalspawn saga's oldest law (kin kill kin) dressed as salvation.
- **The redemption out (synthesis):** if **Drenwal makes *himself* the last sacrifice** — dies and is reborn (Mordenkainen's path) while bearing the sigils — he **breaks** the cycle instead of completing it. Senna lives; he dies and returns clean; Bhaal is denied the kinslaying.

## Evolution of the Sigil (two roads)
- **The Kept Watch** (Coram/discipline) → mastery as a *tool* → **The Severed Blood** (Tier III, post-rebirth: keeps the lethality, no crack risk, capstone **Companion's Judgment**). The salvation ending.
- **The Broken Leash** (Bhaal/indulgence) → raw power → **The Ascendant** (he becomes a Dumal). The damnation ending.
- **Advance via Marks** (3 + the meter threshold per tier). *Watch Marks:* deny a Bhaal/fiend save, spare/protect, re-consecrate with Veska, strike the cult. *Blood Marks:* nova spam, kill greedily, Bhaal climbs, relapse.
- **Catalysts:** Veska (re-consecrate; can scrub Blood Marks if he repents), Companion-light / **Tears of the Caged Star**, hunting the cult, Coram devotionals, Lulu.
- **Hard limit:** **Tears of the Caged Star (raw celestial power) BURNS Drenwal** — the Bhaal blood rejects it and flares. He can't cleanse himself with it. The cure stays the rebirth.

## The possession beat (reference — resolved in play)
Failed CON saves → Bhaal seized his body → he attacked Aurora → she completed the sigil with a quill. **Run any relapse the same way:** Drenwal becomes a DM-controlled Bhaal-puppet (not dying — *possessed*) until subdued or the rite is completed.
