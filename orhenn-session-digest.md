## 2. Or'Henn — the character

**Concept:** Elderly Red Dragonborn druid, ~60–70, native to Avernus. Blind scarred left eye (pale grey) + molten-gold right eye. Infernal-adapted druid armor (leather/bark/ashwood + blackened iron & dark brass, living hellflowers growing from it). Round ashwood shield bearing the heraldic crest of an Avernus devil noble house. Living-ashwood quarterstaff with a floating ember orb.

**Backstory (revised per review):** Or'Henn was hatched in the **Ashen Garden**, a chartered enclave of living retainers attached to the estate of an Avernian noble. The household preserved mortal gardeners, handlers, and scribes because living hands could cultivate things that Hell can corrupt but not create. His predecessor — an enslaved druid taken from the Material Plane — taught him to drive roots through iron, bone, and ash, and left him **Cinderbloom, the badge of the estate's chief keeper and the key to its menagerie wards**. When a **Yeenoghu-aligned warband** (a vanguard, not the demon lord himself — demons reform in the Abyss) breached the estate, Or'Henn fled without the staff. **He retained his druidic magic**, but lost his office, his authority over the surviving beasts, and any lawful claim to the Ashen Garden. He now intends to recover Cinderbloom, settle or weaponize the estate's remaining obligations, and establish a domain of his own. (The staff is his *title and inheritance*, not his class sheet — this preserves his agency.)

**Build (locked):** **Circle of Wildfire** druid, Level 11 (matches the ember theme). Wild Shape cap set by DM ruling to **CR 2** (RAW Wildfire caps at CR 1); non-Beast forms must be earned individually (physical shape + natural attacks only).

**Open plot hooks:**
1. Recover the lost staff (Cinderbloom) — breadcrumb trail in the contracts below.
2. Which devil noble house did he serve? (the shield crest) — candidate: tie it to the demon incursion the party just beat (Yeenoghu), bolting his backstory onto the Session-7 victory.
3. The demons that razed his estate may be the same front the party fought.

## 3. Magic item — Cinderbloom, the Ashen Shepherd

*Staff, **very rare** (attunement by a druid); upgrades to **legendary** ("Cinderbloom Rekindled") via the Mother Ash quest below.* The staff's effects use **the wielder's spell save DC**. It grants **+2 to spell *attack* rolls** (not to save DCs — the +2-to-DC version was pulled back per review). **Revised per review:** Cinderheart now costs **2 charges**; Keeper of Fell Beasts costs **4 charges**, only affects creatures of **CR ≤ your proficiency bonus**, and the target re-saves whenever it takes damage; charges recharge only **once per 24h**. Living-ashwood quarterstaff; grain twists into a cage of blackened branches cradling a fist-sized ember that burns without fuel or smoke. Magic quarterstaff + druidic focus.

- **Ashwood Weapon.** +2 magic quarterstaff (+2 attack & damage) and **+2 to spell attack rolls** while held. (No save-DC bump — kept in line with the rare Staff of the Woodlands; base rarity Very Rare rests on the kit as a whole.)
- **Cinderheart.** Bonus action, expend 1 charge: ember streaks to a point within 60 ft. One creature within 5 ft makes a **DC 17 Dexterity** save, taking **3d8 fire** (half on success). A 10-ft radius of smoldering roots becomes difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. Ignites unattended flammables; never harms plants you choose. *(Balance note: slightly above SotW's offensive budget — dial to 2 charges or 2d8 if it overshadows cantrips.)*
- **Rootwright Magic.** 10 charges. Spell save **DC 17**: *Entangle* (1), *Spike Growth* (2), *Erupting Earth* (3), *Plant Growth* (3), *Wall of Fire* (4). Entangle/spike growth/overgrowth can erupt black ashwood roots from bare stone, metal, bone, or blasted Avernus soil — no vegetation required; crumbles to ash after 8 hours.
- **Recharge.** Regains **1d6+4** charges on the attuned bearer's long rest. Last charge → roll d20; on a **1** the ember goes dark and it becomes a nonmagical staff until replanted in fertile soil and watered during a long rest.
- **Keeper of Fell Beasts.** Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) vs Beasts, Monstrosities, or Fiends of Int ≤ 6. Action + 3 charges: present the ember to one such creature within 30 ft; **DC 17 Wisdom** save (advantage if hostile) or **charmed 1 hour** as trusted keeper, obeying simple bonus-action commands unless suicidal. Ends if you/allies damage it; 24-hr immunity after.

## 4. The Emporium soul-bounties (relayed by Asimov, after recruitment)

> **Revised per review (2026-07-19):** now framed as **proposed instruments** — not binding until Or'Henn marks them in ash, the issuer countersigns, and **Fhet'Ahla** (Mahadi's amnizu notary) seals them; each card carries an Execution/Voiding clause. Rewards renamed *Consideration*, catches renamed *Encumbrance*. Fixes: "two days east" → the Weeping Furrow's three supernatural signs (Avernus has no fixed distances); Serephine's deal notarized as a chartered (elemental) instrument, "+1 focus" = +1 spell attack, "replacement soul" → three soul coins; Morrow via a 500 gp collar-escrow; Contract IV retitled *Articles of Temporary Embodiment* with defined injury-transfer (½ HP Asimov lost as necrotic, max 8d10, never below 1); Malfius demoted to Fhet'Ahla's clerk (not a second amnizu), writ scoped to Zariel's legions/Mahadi's charter, name-substitution bound to the Third Chancery. **The HTML cards are canonical**; the text below is the earlier draft, kept for diffing.

> *The soul capacitor grows warm. Asimov's voice arrives beneath turning pages, clinking coins, distant screams: "Good news: the Emporium has leads. Bad news: every lead has read the fine print."*

### 1 — The Gardener's Due
- **Issuer:** Procurator Vhalzareth, Estate-Reclamation Advocate of the Third Infernal Chancery.
- **Ask:** Recover the estate's brass boundary seal from beneath the demolished western conservatory, intact and "substantially free of demonic residue."
- **Reward:** An authenticated Blood War salvage manifest — the ember-staff was removed by a barlgura called **Kharzug the Root-Eater**, last deployed to the **Weeping Furrow** trench, two days east of the Emporium.
- **Catch:** Legally affirms Or'Henn as final acting groundskeeper → he assumes liability for "all escaped, unculled, or improperly buried specimens." Three still alive; each day of freedom is a separate breach.

### 2 — A Measure of Honest Fire
- **Issuer:** Serephine Coal-Tongue, salamander smith, the Gilded Crucible.
- **Ask:** Enter a rival's warehouse, identify grave-cold iron ingots poisoning her forge, mark them with druidic flame. No theft, no employee slain.
- **Reward:** Sheath one nonmagical weapon/focus in infernal brass (+1) **or** three cinderseed ampoules (thrown, *entangle* in a 20-ft square, 1 min, DC 15).
- **Catch:** "No employee" excludes contractors, debtors, summoned creatures, animated tools, retroactively-terminated. Every marked object is certified defective; if one ingot proves sound, he owes a year as furnace-tender or a replacement soul of equal seniority.

### 3 — The Hound That Remembers
- **Issuer:** Pellucid Jax, beast broker, Jax's Immaculate Menagerie.
- **Ask:** Capture alive and unmaimed an ember-muzzled hell hound ("Morrow") in a green-copper collar; it's eaten three customers and one irreplaceable ledger.
- **Reward:** The collar — inner surface bears his former master's estate crest and a flake of living ashwood that, held over flame, bends toward the staff's location as a **1-hour compass** before burning away.
- **Catch:** The hound is an "independent commercial predator," not Jax's property → buy it *from itself* for 500 gp on delivery. Non-payment transfers the hound and all its prior killings to Or'Henn.

### 4 — Terms of Temporary Resurrection
- **Issuer:** Mistress Nymira Quill, night hag, broker of posthumous opportunities.
- **Ask:** To let Asimov leave the capacitor for one hour, obtain the still-warm shadow of a creature that willingly took an infernal bargain and died regretting it; fold it in before the corpse cools.
- **Reward:** Asimov manifests 1 hr (normal stats/gear as spectral facsimiles); can touch one creature and learn the exact wording of every infernal contract binding it; may name one hidden clause in his own confinement agreement, if one exists.
- **Catch:** A rental, not a resurrection. Injury to the temp body transfers to the nearest *willing* creature at hour's end — "willing" includes anyone who knowingly accepted his healing, inspiration, protection, food, or tactical advice during the hour.

### 5 — The Orchard of Borrowed Names
- **Issuer:** Advocate Malfius Nine-Reeds, an amnizu in a curtained stall with no visible entrance.
- **Ask:** Cut a path through a grove of iron trees (feeding on true names) to a buried infernal war machine; retrieve the obsidian coffer from its command seat. Don't open it or speak your own name inside the grove.
- **Reward:** A **writ of reclamation** (honored by devils below pit-fiend rank) naming Cinderbloom as Or'Henn's property, letting him seize it from any holder. Malfius knows the staff passed through the Weeping Furrow and was given as tribute to **Lady Urexiel, Keeper of the Scabrous Menagerie**.
- **Catch:** The writ establishes ownership by inheritance — naming Or'Henn legal successor to his master's estate, transferring every surviving asset, servant, enemy, debt, oath, and unfinished punishment. Open the coffer (even by accident) and the first true name released replaces Or'Henn's in every contract he's ever signed.

### Recovery arc (breadcrumb)
Contracts 1 → 3 → 5 form the staff-recovery questline:
**estate boundary seal → salvage manifest → Kharzug the Root-Eater (barlgura) → the Weeping Furrow trench → tribute to Lady Urexiel, Keeper of the Scabrous Menagerie.** The ashwood-flake (contract 3) is the tracking compass; the writ (contract 5) is the legal crowbar. Run in that order for a clean 3-beat chase to a boss (Urexiel). **Capstone — the Mother Ash:** after recovering the staff, Or'Henn upgrades it to Legendary by gathering three **Green Remnants** (the last living scraps of Avernus's nature — one held in Urexiel's Scabrous Menagerie, dovetailing with Contract 5) and performing an 8-hour infusion rite at **Vaelisha, the Mother Ash** — the last living tree of Avernus, from whose boughs every infernal gardener's staff was cut. Yields **Cinderbloom Rekindled** (14 charges; Cinderheart 4d8, radiant vs fiends; adds Wall of Thorns / Sunbeam / Fire Storm; once-daily *Bloom of the Last Green* healing/anti-fiend grove; two beasts charmed, Int ≤ 8).

## 9. Tooling notes

- **codex CLI** on this Windows box needs the prompt on **stdin** (piped/background), not as an arg, or it runs empty ("Reading additional input from stdin..."). Plain output, not `--json` parser, is most reliable.
- **No native text-to-image tool.** Options: `fal-ai-media` skill (Flux, needs `FAL_KEY`), MidJourney (manual), or HTML/CSS → PNG via Playwright (used for the cards).
- **PDF:** no Playwright PDF tool; used Edge headless `--print-to-pdf` (preserves hyperlinks).
