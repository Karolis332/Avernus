Beat 1 — The Tower Dragons

Design note: the four tower dragons below are tuned DOWN from their 5e Monster Manual "young dragon" baseline (Black CR7 / Blue CR9 / Green CR8 / Red CR10, drawn from memory, not re-verified against a local MM file) — HP cut, legendary resistance removed, STR raised to push their to-hit up (proficiency bonus held at +3; all numbers below are re-derived from each dragon's own ability scores + that +3, including each breath DC — per the MM pattern, acid/lightning/fire breaths save vs. Dex, poison/cold save vs. Con). Goal: a resource tax on the Lady Vengeance and the party, not a kill fight. They split attacks between the ship and PCs. End trigger (first to occur): the first dragon hits 0 HP, OR the dragons are clearly winning, OR round 4 ends — whichever fires first, Krull (riding Obatala) calls the halt.

Senses darkvision 60 ft. (sees invisible creatures to the same range), passive Perception 10

Special Equipment. Wields Fane-Eater, a +3 battleaxe, and wears Obsidian Flint Dragon Plate (advantage on checks/saves to avoid or end being grappled). Fane-Eater and the Hand of Vecna are never loot — they do not come off his body, dead or alive.

Aura of Hate. While not incapacitated, Arkhan and all fiends/undead within 30 ft. deal +4 damage on melee weapon hits (already folded into the melee numbers below; it does not apply to the ranged Javelin).

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If Arkhan fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead. This is a free trait, usable the instant he'd fail a save — it is not a reaction and does not compete with his legendary actions.

Hand of Vecna (12 charges, regains 2d4+4 at dawn). Spell save DC 20 (8 + prof 7 + Cha +5). finger of death (5), slow (2), teleport (3), sleep (1). Vecna's Grip (signature, 4 charges): one creature within 60 ft., DC 20 Con save or restrained and silenced until the start of Arkhan's next turn (the Hand closes bodily around the target's throat from across the room) — his answer to a caster or a fleeing PC.

Innate Spellcasting. Charisma (spell save DC 20, +12 to hit [prof +7, Cha +5]). 3/day each: animate dead, branding smite, revivify. 1/day each: geas, raise dead.

Actions

Multiattack. Arkhan makes three Fane-Eater attacks.

Fane-Eater (Battleaxe). +15 to hit (Str +5, prof +7, weapon +3), reach 5 ft. Hit: 16 (1d8 + 12) slashing, or 17 (1d10 + 12) slashing if used two-handed, plus 9 (2d8) cold [the +12 = Str +5, weapon +3, Aura +4]. On a natural 20: extra 9 (2d8) necrotic, and Arkhan regains that much HP.

Javelin. Ranged Weapon Attack: +12 to hit (Str +5, prof +7; not magical, no Aura), range 30/120 ft. Hit: 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing. Used in melee instead (reach 5 ft.), it benefits from Aura of Hate: same +12 to hit, 12 (1d6 + 5 + 4 Aura) piercing.

Legendary Actions (3/round)

Only one at a time, only at the end of another creature's turn. Regains all 3 at the start of his turn.

Strike (Costs 1). One Fane-Eater attack (the one-handed line above: 16 (1d8+12) slashing plus 9 (2d8) cold).

Hand of Vecna (Costs 2 or 3, per spell cost above). Cast a Hand spell.

Command the Line (Costs 1). Arkhan directs Torogar, Krull, the manticore, or a dragon: that ally gains advantage on its next attack, or may use its reaction early.

Lair Action — the Monument only (Initiative count 20, losing ties)

The Five-Headed Statue Breathes. Once per round, Tiamat's statue exhales through one head in a 30-ft. cone aimed only at the party and their allies, never at Arkhan's side. DC 16 save, type and ability matching the head per the MM pattern — white (cold): Constitution, black (acid): Dexterity, green (poison): Constitution, blue (lightning): Dexterity, red (fire): Dexterity — 21 (6d6) damage of that type, half on a success.

CR math (one line). Offensive: 3 Fane-Eater attacks @ +15 (~25 dmg each = ~75 DPR) plus a 4th via one legendary Strike (~25 more, ~100) — or up to ~150 if all 3 legendary actions go to Strike in one round — puts him solidly in the CR 22+ offensive band. Defensive: AC 23 / HP 345, but Legendary Resistance (3/Day) converts his first 3 "would-fail" saves into successes, which the DMG's effective-HP method treats as roughly +85–95 HP of staying power → ~440 effective HP at AC 23, matching the CR 22 defensive band. Tag: CR 22.

Devil's Sight. Magical darkness doesn't impede the abishai's darkvision.

Magic Resistance. The abishai has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Magic Weapons. The abishai's weapon attacks are magical.

Reckless. At the start of its turn, the abishai can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.

Actions

Multiattack. The abishai makes two attacks: one with its longsword and one with its claws.

Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used two-handed.

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

Reactions

Vicious Reprisal. In response to taking damage, the abishai makes a bite attack against a random creature within 5 feet of it. If no creature is in reach, the abishai moves up to half its speed toward an enemy it can see, without provoking opportunity attacks.

At the table. They do not move from the skull-trophies unless the party is clearly winning the Showdown — then 1d4+1 drop in to tip the scales back toward Arkhan.
