The Ashen Shepherd's Codex of Fell Shapes — kept in Or'Henn's own hand · Circle of Wildfire · Druid 11 of Avernus —

I kept a garden in Hell once, and beasts within it — fed them, named them, mended their hurts. The garden is ash now and the beasts are pages. What I could not carry out, I learned to wear. Read gently; some of these I loved. — Or'Henn, called the Ashen Shepherd

Forms of Avernus available to the Ashen Shepherd · each name opens its 5e.tools stat block

The rule at this level (2024). Or'Henn uses Wild Shape as a Bonus Action. He walks the Circle of Wildfire — which grants no CR boost, so by the book he would cap at CR 1. By DM ruling, his long keeper's training among infernal beasts lets him assume Beast and earned-infernal forms of CR 2 or lower. CR 3 forms are beyond his reach. Wild Shape normally permits Beasts only — the BEAST forms are always legal. Every EARNED form (Monstrosity, Fiend, or Elemental) is a campaign-specific shape granted through Or'Henn's infernal training and his bond with Cinderbloom.
Unlocking infernal forms. Or'Henn does not automatically gain every eligible non-Beast form. Each must be unlocked — by studying, defeating, binding, or tending that specific creature — and he gains only its physical shape and natural attacks. Innate Spellcasting, Shapechanger, Summon, and similar magical traits are not gained unless the DM explicitly grants them. This list is campaign progression, not a power grant. An EARNED shape is a memory paid for, not a gift.
