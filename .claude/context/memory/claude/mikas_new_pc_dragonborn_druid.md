Player **Mikas** (previously ran **Asimov**, Soulknife Rogue — see roster in [[session_handoff_yeenoghu]]) swaps to a new character, **Or'Henn**, as of Session 7/8. **Asimov is NOT dead:** his soul is held in a **soul capacitor**; the party travels to the **Wandering Emporium** (Mahadi's market) to find him a **patron**. **Continuity (locked):** **Asimov was already a party member** (Mikas's original PC) who **DIED to Yeenoghu** in the Session-7 climax; his soul was captured in the **soul capacitor**. **Or'Henn is the fresh recruit** (Mikas's new PC) and **never knew Asimov in life** — the party finds/recruits Or'Henn at the Wandering Emporium, where he's a **post-ravager refugee** (supplies, shelter, word of other estates to serve/claim/rebuild). From the capacitor, Asimov's trapped soul relays the Emporium jobs — reframed as **soul-bounties** — to Or'Henn (bridge between the party's fallen PC and its new one). The **Yeenoghu-aligned warband** that razed Or'Henn's estate is the same front that killed Asimov — a shared grievance.

**Soul capacitor & the Soul Council (the engine):** the capacitor is a bounty-hunter's soul-vessel governed by the **Soul Council**. Asimov's soul occupies it now **in place of Rasheem**, the **previous occupant** — and the one who **fed Asimov his bounties** when Asimov was the living hunter. The role runs in a chain: the trapped soul relays bounties to a living bounty hunter, and when that hunter dies they take the occupant's place (Asimov's death to Yeenoghu is why the vessel holds him now, not Rasheem — and why Asimov relays to Or'Henn/the party as Rasheem once did to him). The Council has given Asimov **one week (in-fiction) to find another bounty hunter** — the ticking clock behind the party recruiting Or'Henn and the **soul-bounties** Asimov relays from the vessel.

**New PC concept (from Mikas's portrait prompt, WOTC-5e illustration style):**
- **Elderly Red Dragonborn druid**, ~60–70, visibly aged: faded crimson scales, chipped horns, cracked muzzle, stooped but imposing.
- One eye **blind** (pale grey, old diagonal scar); other eye **molten gold**. Stern.
- **Infernal-adapted druid armor**: leather + bark + ash wood with blackened-iron/dark-brass infernal accents; living **hellish flowers** (ember petals, glowing veins, black stems) growing from it.
- **Shield**: aged ash wood + infernal metal bands, boss engraved with the **heraldic crest of an Avernus devil noble house** (relic — plot hook: which house?).
- **Quarterstaff**: single grown ash-wood piece, unpolished, spiraling living-wood top cradling a floating **crimson-orange ember orb** (burns without consuming).
- **Backdrop**: a cultivated **infernal estate garden in Avernus** — charred-yet-living trees, molten crimson flowerbeds, ash drifting; a master druid who nurtures hellish life.

**Backstory (Or'Henn) — revised per codex review 2026-07-19:** Hatched in the **Ashen Garden**, a chartered enclave of living retainers attached to an Avernian noble's estate (the household kept mortal gardeners/handlers because living hands can cultivate what Hell corrupts but cannot create — explains an Avernus-born dragonborn). Trained by his predecessor, an enslaved druid taken from the Material Plane, who left him **Cinderbloom** — the **badge of the estate's chief keeper and key to its menagerie wards**, NOT his class powers. When a **Yeenoghu-aligned warband** (a vanguard — not Yeenoghu himself; demons reform in the Abyss) breached the estate, he fled without the staff. **He keeps his ordinary druid magic**; what he lost is his office, his authority over the surviving beasts, and his lawful claim to the Ashen Garden. Wants to recover Cinderbloom, settle or weaponize the estate's remaining obligations, and build his own domain. (Dropped the old "no staff = no powers" — it killed his agency.)

**Plot hooks:** (1) recover the lost staff — homebrew item being drafted (see [[booklet_pdf_rendering]] for the eventual artifact); Staff of the Woodlands is the closest published analog; (2) which **devil noble house** he served (the shield crest), possibly tied to the Session-7 Yeenoghu/demon incursion; (3) the demons that razed his estate may be the same front the party just fought — direct link to their victory.
