Adopted 2026-07-25 after a codex canon-review of [[descent_avernus_master_primer]]. These refine published DiA canon into the homebrew (hybrid — kept the homebrew, adopted the canon gold):

- **The CONTRACT damned Elturel, NOT the Sword.** The Sword of Zariel was carried out of the Hells by the paladin **Yael** + the hollyphant **Lulu**, who sacrificed life/magic/memory to raise the **Bleeding Citadel** to hide it. **★ Lulu is in the party — her recovering fragmentary memories are the literal path to the Citadel/Sword** (the emotional key of the endgame). **Olanthius** = a guilty WITNESS / route-holder (crime is silence, not theft); the **Eldra Vash signet** is the key to talking him down.
- **Contract copy vs original:** Kreeg's puzzle box (in Urexiel's vault) = a **COPY** = the *legal* lever (copy + Kreeg's **living** hand + an infernal writ → void/renegotiate the sale). **Zariel holds the ORIGINAL** = the *physical* lever (destroy → Elturel's chains break).
- **Victory tiers:** one lever alone = a compromised ending · **Sword + Contract = the clean win** · **+ Coram = the redemptive ending**.
- **Lady Urexiel = a YUGOLOTH** broker who **commissioned the raid** that razed the Ashen Garden + killed Asimov (she is the paymaster; Kreeg was the target/fugitive). Keeps Blood War logic (demon loot → yugoloth → devil buyers).
- **Kreeg's motive:** desperate savior (took Zariel's "salvation" during an undead crisis) → bound the city via the **Creed Resolute** → fled before collection. A **living signatory** has unique legal value — the party can LEARN this before any chance to kill him (no gotcha). See [[thavius_kreeg_canon]].

Primer modules M2/M3/M6/M8 were regenerated to this canon; the errata "CANON UPDATES" box governs the rest of the doc.
