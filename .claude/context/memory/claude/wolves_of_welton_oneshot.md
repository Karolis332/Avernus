**Key design notes (do not re-derive):**
- **The reveal:** wolves are sapient. Caused by a Wild Magic Surge from Welton's missing sorcerer Alexi Merriksonn, which dumped his intellect + elemental power into the pack.
- **Featherock is the keystone clue.** Halfling shepherd whose testimony "the wolves were talking" sets up Scene 5.
- **Two alpha wolves with opposing goals:** Flame (chaotic, fire breath, wants war) vs Bolt (lawful, lightning breath, wants peace). They will kill each other if PCs don't intervene.
- **Peace Trigger** fires when: 4 wolves killed OR alpha < half HP OR any PC at 0 HP. Bolt then roars "HOLD!" Players can negotiate or ignore.
- **The Flame Betrayal** is the real climax — Flame attacks Bolt the moment peace looks likely.

**Default tuning is for 2nd-3rd level × 4-5 PCs.**
- For **4 PCs at level 2**: too lethal. Default Flame Fire Breath (2d8, DC 13) will TPK level-2 wizards in one round.
- Use the **Level-2 tuned statblocks** in the primer/HTML: Flame/Bolt HP 35, breath 2d6, DC 12, gated to round 2+, only one alpha breaths per round, drop Scene 5 wolves from 10 to 6, lower Peace Trigger threshold to 3 wolves killed, **skip Scene 4 (Owlbear)**.
- Or bump party to level 3 before running and use defaults.
