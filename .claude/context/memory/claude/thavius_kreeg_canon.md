Karolis directive (2026-07-25). **Thavius Kreeg** (canon DiA High Overseer who sold Elturel to Zariel) is the **estate-keeper Or'Henn served.** The **Ashen Garden** — Or'Henn's razed home — is Kreeg's private Avernian estate.

**Kreeg is ALIVE.** He fled the Ashen Garden through a pre-paid infernal bolthole when the raid razed it, and now hides at the **Wandering Emporium** as **"Vessper Hain,"** a stooped soul-coin appraiser paying **Mahadi** for anonymity. Bind: **Zariel wants his soul** (his safe-passage letter expired when Elturel fell).

**Motive arc (adopted):** desperate savior → traitor — he took Zariel's horrific "salvation" during an undead crisis, bound the city through the **Creed Resolute**, then fled before collection. A **living signatory** has unique legal value; the party can learn this before any chance to kill him (**no gotcha**).

**The contract (see [[elturel_endgame_canon]]):** Kreeg's puzzle box in **Lady Urexiel's** vault holds a **COPY** = the legal lever to void the sale (needs his living hand + a writ); **Zariel holds the ORIGINAL** whose destruction breaks Elturel's chains. **Lady Urexiel is a yugoloth** who commissioned the raid (the paymaster; Kreeg was the target).

**Timeline canon:** the estate is **decades old** — Kreeg's pact + Hell-side holdings **predate** Elturel's actual fall; absentee owner (Or'Henn saw him once, robed, at distance). Or'Henn's ~60 yrs service + Eldra's grave are consistent; **Eldra was long-lived/fey-preserved**.

**Emotional payoff:** Or'Henn unknowingly served the arch-traitor who damned Drenwal's city and killed his father's order. Table-silencing reveal ("The Neighbor," primer M8). Or'Henn's predecessor Eldra = an enslaved Material-Plane druid who tended the garden, taught him, left him Cinderbloom.
