The DM bought Higgsfield Plus on 2026-11-12 (1200 credits) for campaign video clips; the tool is the official CLI `@higgsfield/cli` (`higgsfield`, `higgs`, `hf`), signed in on this PC. Never run `higgsfield auth token` (prints the token) and strip emails from `account status` output.

**Recipe that works** (first clip: Obatala's Cold Breath, job `76b95a7c-b7ea-4423-97a0-87b57368a758`):
1. Start frame = a Midjourney still as jpg (candidates in `dm-app/docs/style-test/mj/`). `higgsfield upload create <file>` or reuse an id from `upload list --image`.
2. `higgsfield generate create kling4_0 --mode image-to-video --start-image <upload_id> --duration 5 --resolution 720p --aspect_ratio auto --prompt "..." --json` prints the job id at once.
3. `higgsfield generate wait <id> --timeout 9m --interval 10s --quiet --json` (about 3.5 min), then curl `result_url`.
4. Re-encode for the repo: `ffmpeg -c:v libx264 -crf 25 -preset slow -movflags +faststart -c:a aac -b:a 96k` (8.4 MB to 1.25 MB), save as `dm-app/art/generated/<id>-<move>.mp4`, add `video:` beside `art:` on the action.

**Traps:** two `generate create ... --wait` calls (local file path, 1080p) were each charged 16.25 credits and printed `[]` / "request failed (no response received)", so no job id was captured. Do not use `--wait` on create. `generate list` never shows Kling jobs (only `generate get <id>` finds them), so save every id to a file at once; those two clips may exist in the web library. Plus allows 6 `kling4_0` jobs at once; a 7th create is refused (`rate_limit_reached`) and not charged. The CLI has no cancel.

**Restyle a still first** when the picture is wrong: `generate create nano_banana_2_1 --image <upload_id> --aspect_ratio auto --resolution 2k --prompt "Edit this painting. Keep the same dragon, pose, composition and painterly style. Change ..."` = 2 credits, under a minute; the job id works as `--start-image` for Kling.

**Art direction (DM, 2026-11-12):** Obatala is "she", she is in the Hells (Avernus backdrop, never snowfields), "her scales are melting due to the heat", and everything should be "more grandiose, it is an ancient dragon" (full-body wide shots, tiny figures for scale). "The dragon's face needs to be the same" in every picture and "the cold breath version of the face is perfect": that face is `dm-app/docs/style-test/obatala-face-reference.webp`; pass it as the second `--image` when painting any Obatala still. Front views drift whiter and thinner, so say "broad, heavy, blunt-snouted, weathered grey-blue". The Kling-generated sounds are approved.

**Batch rule:** twice a batch of six was rendering when the DM's correction arrived (135 credits of drafts, no cancel). The DM steers in real time: after any change of look, show stills or one clip and wait for a go before a batch.

**Wanted next (DM):** a cast board, like a soundboard: a player-facing window on a second monitor, and a DM board of buttons with previews; one click casts the clip or still to the player window.

**Quality bar (DM, 2026-11-12):** the 720p clips from painted stills "look a lot low quality": "i need details", "like proper cinematics". Cause measured: at 720p in a wide shot the subject is a few hundred pixels and the detail smears; the app re-encode was not the cause. Cinematic recipe (sample `cine-cold-breath.mp4`, job `5c6418a1-6263-490b-a3a2-e8a3dd26897b`): `cinematic_studio_2_5` still at 4k with the face reference (4 credits, 5504x3072, photoreal film still; it painted a grey sky, fixed with a `nano_banana_2_1` 4k edit, 3 credits), then `kling4_0` at 1080p (16.25 per 5 s) with "Cinematic shot, low angle, very slow push-in ... photorealistic, highly detailed". Dearer tiers not yet tried: `cinematic_studio_video_4_0` 1080p = 80 credits per 5 s (160 for 10 s); `topaz_video` upscale to 2160p (no cost estimate available). Never render 720p again for this DM.

**Cinematic set (2026-11-12):** the DM picked the cinematic sample ("left one good") with one fix: "the soldiers in the background dont fit in to the theme". No generic soldiers or extras in any frame; write "No people, no soldiers" and let rock spires give scale (only scene figures: Krull the tortle rider, the Mirror's devils and three cloaked adventurers). Lessons from the eleven stills: a 5.5K job id fails as a `cinematic_studio_2_5` reference (seven jobs failed, uncharged), so upload a 2048 px jpg (`cine-dragon-ref.jpg`); it paints grey skies about one time in three and copies a painted reference's style, so drop painted references and fix skies with a `nano_banana_2_1` 4k edit (3 credits); check every still for sky, face, leg count and stray chains before paying 16.25 for the clip. All eleven clips are `cine-*.mp4` in `.playwright-mcp/hf/` (job ids in `clips.json`).

**Motion and figures (DM, 2026-11-12, after seeing the eleven cinematic clips in the app):** "these are looking great, maybe more motion in the dragon but it does feel heavy and powerful, I like that". Keep the weight ("heavy, slow, powerful, the ground shakes"), but give the creature two or three big body actions in a row and hold the camera close ("the camera never pulls back"): the Frightful Presence rerun done this way was clearly better. "for the mirror of mephistar the devils look completely like stick figures": small figures in a wide shot always come out as sticks, so move the camera in until each creature is large in frame and describe its anatomy. My reading, not the DM's words: a shot only works when its subject fills at least a third of the frame.

**Stills that needed a second pass (2026-11-12):** `cinematic_studio_2_5` turns unfamiliar monsters into armoured knights or plain skeletons; a `nano_banana_2_1` 4k edit that names the anatomy fixes it and keeps the composition (`.playwright-mcp/hf/edits.py`, 3 credits). For forms of one creature, generate the best one first and pass it as the reference for the others. Kling pricing is linear: 3.25 credits per second at 1080p, 2.25 at 720p, clips of 5, 10 or 15 s; it also takes `end_image`, `image_references` and `kling_element_ids`.

**DM's order of work (2026-11-12):** the 3-minute fight film of the Session 13 sky chase first, then all of Dumal's attacks and transformations.

**Fight films (DM, 2026-11-12):** a single slow image-to-video shot was "quite bad", "very static": "resemble vox machina in the way it changes scenes and fluidly does it", with one six-second block per combat round ("so 6s a round", "thats why things need to happen in 6 seconds intervals"). What worked first try: `kling4_0 --mode reference-to-video --duration 6` (19.5 credits at 1080p), up to four `--image-references` (ship, dragons, heroes), and a prompt of the form "edited like a fast animated action series: rapid hard cuts, whip pans ... Three quick shots, two seconds each, with hard cuts between them. Shot 1: ... Shot 2: ... Shot 3: ..." naming each cast member as "the X from the second reference image". Check the first frames of each clip: one opened with a flash of the reference image (trim it). Driver `film.py` in `dm-app/docs/style-test/higgsfield/`.

**Fight film v2 (DM on the photoreal three-cut v1, 2026-11-12):** "the idea is good" but "need more fluidity", "more combat from the characters", "their looks need to be unique", "try to replicate the flow of vox machina and animation stylization without the voice acting". So fight films are 2D animated, not photoreal (the stat block clips stay photoreal: the DM approved those). Recipe: redraw each photoreal reference with a `nano_banana_2_1` 2k edit (2 credits) whose prompt opens "Redraw this image as a frame from a modern 2D hand-drawn adult animated fantasy action series: clean ink outlines, flat cel-shaded colours ..." (never name the show or copy its characters), then Kling `reference-to-video` with those references and a prompt for "one continuous flowing take" that passes the action from hero to hero by whip pans and follows a projectile, ending "No dialogue, no voices". The heroes must act in every round, not just the dragons and the ship. Reference backgrounds leak into the clip (masts and rigging did): keep them plain or correct. Hero looks from the DM are in `campaign-state.md` (Session 13, Looks for art). Kling drew the Lady Vengeance with white sails in a wide shot it invented: for any exterior of the ship, start an image-to-video shot from the ship still instead (3 s, 9.75 credits) and cut it in. Clips can differ by a few pixels (1928x1076): scale every input to 1920x1080 before the concat filter.

**Costs measured:** `kling4_0` image-to-video 5 s: 720p 11.25, 1080p 16.25. `seedance_2_5` 5 s 720p 35. `veo3_1` fast 6 s 24. Price a call for free with `generate cost`.

**Prompt pattern the DM's feedback produced:** "Static shot, fixed camera, no camera movement" + the creature stays planted + one or two clear actions (inhale, then a wide spreading cone) + "Keep the gritty painterly dark fantasy illustration style of the image". The DM rejected a code-drawn clip ("stick figure") and a beam that "looks like a leash". Related: [[free-tools-only]].

**Look of the fight films, as of 2026-11-13:** the DM moved off the cel-shaded look ("AI slop faces") and picked **painterly** (style B on `face-styles.webp`): no outlines, brushed light and colour, realistic bone structure, dark horror mood. No spoken hero lines (a test was "hella cringe"): screams, dread and the place carry the sound. To restyle a reference, pass two images to `nano_banana_2_1` (the character, then a crop of the chosen style) and say the second is only a style reference; an edit with a style description alone barely changes the picture. Plus runs 8 image jobs at once. Paint a key frame per shot, get it approved, then animate with image-to-video from that frame. Six seconds holds about three beats: a fourth gets cut off.

**Never treat an ambiguous message as a go for a batch (2026-11-13):** the DM wrote "show me the video" while a storyboard was waiting; I started six rounds (117 credits), then "wait" arrived with a different style (panel C, dark anime, "use this artstyle for all"). No cancel exists. Render a batch only on an explicit yes to that batch; otherwise answer that the video does not exist yet and ask. Style C is the DM's pick "for all"; `video-edit` restyling costs 24 credits per 6 s, more than a fresh render.

**Sky chase film finished 2026-11-13 (commit `aa63b23`):** the DM chose to finish the painted (style B) film when a style C recut cost more than the balance: `dm-app/art/generated/sky-chase-film.mp4`, eleven rounds, 65 s, in the S13 scene `sky-chase`. Balance after it: 3.5 credits, so nothing renders until a top-up. Ask which look (B or C) before the next film. Two more traps seen: the background of a character reference leaks into a cut (Aurora's deck-and-volcano reference turned the ground scene into a deck scene and grew a sailing ship), so use references on plain backgrounds; two look-alike armoured characters in one shot morph into each other (Lucan became Veska at the end of round 1; the DM hated it; cut out by hand, film recut in `8c2830d`); `generate wait` can print "failed" while `generate get <id>` still says `in_progress`, so check `get` before treating a job as lost.

**Shots separately, cut by hand (DM asked 2026-11-13, "to not waste tokens"):** render each beat as its own 3 s `kling4_0` clip (the minimum; 9.75 credits at 1080p, the same 3.25 per second as a 6 s clip), trim to 1.5-2.5 s and join with ffmpeg (`join_painted.sh`). A bad beat is retaken alone for half the price and look-alike characters never share a clip. Keep one 6 s take only for a single flowing move. Third-party Kling 4.0 guides also give second ranges in the prompt ("0-2 s: one action, camera move", ranges adding up to the clip length); untested by us.

**Never re-encode over a file the DM has open:** writing the recut straight onto `sky-chase-film.mp4` left it half-written for a minute and he asked why the movie was gone. Encode to a temporary name, then move it into place, and say so first.
