## How to make the Spotify developer app

| # | Step |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with **the Spotify account you play from**. It needs Premium: Spotify only lets Premium accounts own a development app. Accept the developer terms and confirm your email if it asks. |
| 2 | Click **Create app**. **Name:** anything (e.g. `DM Emporium`). **Description:** anything. **Website:** leave empty. |
| 3 | **Redirect URI:** type `http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback` exactly, then press **Add**. |
| 4 | Under the APIs, tick **Web API**, accept the terms and press **Save**. |
| 5 | Open the app, then **Settings**. Copy the **Client ID**, then press **View client secret** and copy that too. |
| 6 | Press Win+R and run `notepad %USERPROFILE%\.spotify-dm\credentials.json`. Replace the two `PASTE ...` placeholders with the ID and the secret, keeping the quotes, and save. |
| 7 | Restart Claude Code, then tell me "spotify ready". I start the Spotify login: a browser tab opens and you press **Agree** once. |
