The Ashen Shepherd

Or'Henn

Elderly Red Dragonborn Druid · Circle of Wildfire · Level 11 · native of Avernus

Who You Are

You were hatched in the Ashen Garden — a chartered enclave of living retainers kept on the estate of an Avernian devil-noble. Hell can corrupt what grows, but it cannot create it; so the household preserved mortal gardeners, handlers, and scribes whose living hands could cultivate what devils could not. You were raised there as gardener and beast-keeper.

Your predecessor — a druid dragged from the Material Plane and bound to the estate — taught you to drive roots through iron, bone, and ash. When he passed, he left you Cinderbloom: the badge of the estate's chief keeper and the key to its menagerie wards.

Then a Yeenoghu-aligned demon warband breached the estate and razed it. You fled — without the staff. You still carry every scrap of your druidic craft; what the fire took was your office, your authority over the surviving beasts, and any lawful claim to the Ashen Garden.

You are old now, roughly sixty-five: crimson scales gone matte, horns chipped, one eye scarred pale-grey and blind, the other a calm, molten gold. Weathered, patient, and not finished yet.

Your Ambition

Recover the staff. Restore it. Reclaim a domain of your own in Hell.

Cinderbloom is your title and your inheritance, not merely a tool. Find it, rekindle it if the old legend is true, and turn the estate's unfinished obligations to your advantage — until the Ashen Garden, or something greater, answers to you.

Cinderbloom, Your Lost Staff

Staff, very rare (requires attunement by a druid) · a magic quarterstaff and druidic focus. Currently lost — this is your reference for the day you get it back.

Living-ashwood, warm as sun-baked stone, its grain twisting up into a cage of blackened branches that cradles a fist-sized ember burning without fuel or smoke — brightening whenever beasts draw near or roots stir underground.

Ashwood Weapon. While you hold it: +2 to attack and damage rolls with the quarterstaff, and +2 to your spell attack rolls.

Cinderheart. Bonus action, 2 charges: hurl the ember to a point within 60 ft. One creature within 5 ft makes a Dexterity save vs your spell save DC, taking 3d8 fire (half on success). Smoldering roots erupt in a 10-ft radius (difficult terrain until your next turn). Ignites loose flammables; never harms plants you choose.

Rootwright Magic. The staff holds 10 charges. Take a Magic action to spend charges and cast, using your spell save DC:

  • Entangle — 1 charge
  • Spike Growth — 2 charges
  • Erupting Earth — 3 charges
  • Plant Growth — 3 charges
  • Wall of Fire — 4 charges

Cast from Cinderbloom, entangle, spike growth, and plant growth can erupt black ashwood roots straight out of bare stone, metal, bone, or blasted Avernus soil — no vegetation needed. Such growth crumbles to ash after 8 hours.

Keeper of Fell Beasts. Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks with Beasts, Monstrosities, or Fiends of Intelligence 6 or lower. Magic action, 4 charges: present the ember to one such creature within 30 ft whose CR is no higher than your proficiency bonus; it makes a Wisdom save vs your spell save DC (advantage if hostile) or is charmed 1 hour as your trusted keeper, obeying simple bonus-action commands unless obviously suicidal. It re-saves whenever it takes damage; when the effect ends it's immune for 24 hours.

Recharge. Regains 1d6 + 4 charges on the attuned bearer's long rest, once every 24 hours. Spend the last charge and roll a d20 — on a 1, the ember darkens and it becomes a plain staff until replanted in fertile soil and watered through a long rest.

The legend of the rekindling. The keepers say Cinderbloom can supposedly be rekindled at the last living tree of Avernus — restored to a far greater power. Whether that tree still stands, and what it would cost, is a story worth chasing. (A goal, not yet a recipe.)

Wild Shape — Your Infernal Menagerie

How it works (2024 rules). You Wild Shape as a Bonus Action. You are a Circle of Wildfire druid — by the book that caps you at CR 1, but your infernal keeper's training (a DM ruling for you) lets you take Beast and earned-infernal forms of challenge rating 2 or lower. CR 3 forms are beyond your reach. The BEAST rows below are always legal.

Your infernal edge (homebrew). You can also wear infernal forms — Monstrosity, Fiend, or Elemental — but only ones you have personally earned by studying, defeating, binding, or tending that specific creature. You gain its physical shape and natural attacks only — no innate spellcasting, no Shapechanger, no summoning. These EARNED forms are a growing collection, not a free list.
BEAST — always legal (RAW) EARNED — Monstrosity / Fiend / Elemental you must unlock in play ★ signature form

How to add a form. Your menagerie grows through play. Study a creature you can watch closely, defeat one in the field, bind one with Cinderbloom's Keeper of Fell Beasts, or tend one back to health — then it may join your list. Tell your DM which creature you're working toward; every hunt is a chance to add a new skin.

At the Wandering Emporium

The ravagers took your estate, not your wits. You made your way to the Wandering Emporium — Mahadi's traveling infernal market — for what a burned-out keeper needs: supplies, shelter, and word of other estates you might serve, claim, or rebuild. Weathered and alone among its stalls and advocates, you are exactly where an adventuring party crosses your path. What they want with an old dragonborn gardener — and what you might want with them — is where your story begins.
